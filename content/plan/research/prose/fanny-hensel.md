## Corrections and omissions

- Removed the precise claim that the Bach-prelude memory performance occurred at thirteen: the consulted chronologies disagree.
- Distinguished the 1841 composition of *Das Jahr* from its illustrated 1842 fair copy, and avoided treating its twelve months as a literal diary of the Italian tour.
- Retained the Gounod encounter without claiming that his memoir itself names Beethoven; the Beethoven episode comes from Fanny’s diary as quoted by Jean-Pierre Gounod.
- No identity fields, ABC strings, image filenames, poem originals, reading entries or lineage arrays changed. Removed `featured`.

### Corrections

- [EXT] Painter known since 1821 → painter, without the meeting year. The [research chronology][H1] gives 1821, but the [custodial archive biography][H2] gives 1822. Removed the disputed precision; the October 1829 marriage is independently secure.
- [EXT] Bach revelation followed by Beethoven → Bach revelation; she also played Beethoven. Neither the [memoir][H3] nor the [dated diary episode][H4] establishes that sequence of introduction.
- [EXT] Gounod’s introduction to Bach and Beethoven collectively → introduction specifically to Bach’s keyboard works. His [memoir][H3], Italy chapter, says that he already knew Beethoven’s symphonies by heart; its Hensel passage names unfamiliar Bach and Mendelssohn works. The [Naxos booklet][H5] explicitly identifies her introduction to Bach’s keyboard music.
- [EXT] Poem published 1826 → poem first published 1823. [Bibliotheca Augustana][H6] distinguishes the first printing from the 1826 version. This changes only the arts caption, not the protected poem original or any composer/work timeline field.
