## Price’s attested words

The header quotation, **“I should like to be judged on merit alone.”**, and the life quotation **“To begin with I have two handicaps — those of sex and race.”** are from Price’s letter to Serge Koussevitzky of **5 July 1943**, transcribed in B. I corroborates the latter sentence; H cites the letter through Rae Linda Brown’s biography, p. 186. Spacing around the dash is house typography. The letter facsimile was not inspected; the published transcriptions are the evidence. No invented dialogue or reconstructed inner thoughts appear. The broken-foot anecdote is paraphrased from B; its amusing letter is not quoted without an established recipient/date.

## Score figures and rights

All three ABC figures are **original, explicitly labelled schematics**, not transcriptions, approximations of Price melodies, or extracts from a modern copyrighted edition.

1. **Minor pentatonic melody:** four invented bars in E minor using only E–G–A–B–D. Demonstrates a five-note collection; B/C describe pentatonic/spiritual-like melodic resources. It makes no claim to reproduce either principal symphonic theme or the Fantasie’s spiritual.
2. **Straight and syncopated rhythm:** four invented bars in C, with a steady quarter-note bass and longer upper notes beginning off the beat in alternate bars. B/C support syncopation as a Juba feature; the particular rhythm is the editor’s teaching example, not a named traditional pattern or Price quotation.
3. **D major / F minor:** four invented bars alternating root-position triads. I identifies these chord types at the opening of the Second Violin Concerto. Durations, spacing and repetition are explicitly invented. The statement that the triads have no common notes follows their spellings D–F-sharp–A and F–A-flat–C.

The project validator accepted all three ABC examples. No MIDI/synthesised performance is supplied as a listening recording.
