## Editorial decisions

No invented speech or unverified anecdote has been retained. The note about Sand’s being a novelist is replaced by the actual circumstances of Delacroix’s portrait. The birthday conflict is told as a conflict between two surviving kinds of evidence, without inventing an explanation for the discrepancy. Schumann’s 1831 praise of Op. 2 is dated, but its famous wording is not quoted here.

### Protected-field findings
