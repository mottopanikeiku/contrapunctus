## Editorial decisions

- No speculative identity for the ‘Immortal Beloved’ or medical cause of deafness.
- The Napoleon episode is retained with the date and status of Ries’s recollection made clear in the margin.
- No unverified famous Beethoven saying was added. The composer’s voice comes from the dated Wegeler letter, the Testament and an autograph score inscription.
