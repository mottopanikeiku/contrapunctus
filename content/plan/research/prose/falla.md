## Notation

All three score examples are invented schematics, labelled as such in kicker, title and caption: the Andalusian cadence (A minor–G–F–E, bass A–G–F–E); E Phrygian with raised third; the six open strings of a guitar at sounding pitch. No Falla bars are quoted. Falla scores were read as page images from public-domain scans (Chester 1921 piano editions; Eschig 1922–23) to confirm markings quoted in Hall notes; transcribing melodic bars from them was judged too uncertain.

### Corrections made

1. **Life, para 1.** Roland-Manuel’s “for six years” attaches to Falla’s governing of Colón, and the opera is “the triumph of the day”, not “of its season”. Rewritten.
2. **Life, Pedrell.** Pahissa p. 35 has “the clearest and most solid guidance for my work”; “he ever had” was an addition. Removed.
3. **Life, first visit to Granada.** The Archivo dates María Lejárraga’s trip to late March and early April 1915, before the première of *El amor brujo* on 15 April; the chapter said “That April” after the première. Rewritten as “in the weeks before the première”; `content/geo/falla.json` entry changed to `from: 1915-03` with the dates in its text.
4. **Sound, “Old music”.** García Lorca says the tissue paper was meant to make the music sound old-fashioned; the harpsichord sound was the result, not the aim. Rewritten (the life text already had it right).
5. **Sound, “Paris colour”.** The Eschig full score (printed p. 3, Internet Archive image) marks the harp *sulla tavola*, the first violins *div. pizz.* and the violas *Tutte / Solo, sul ponticello*, and leaves the piano silent; the cellos’ upper line is written with slurs and ties, a *div. pizz.* mark stands between the cello and bass staves, and *pizz.* is marked for the cellos from the third bar of the next page. A flat “pizzicato cellos and basses” overstated it; the text now says “plucked notes in the first violins and the low strings”. Printed p. 35 confirms movement II: *Allegretto giusto* ♩ = 100, *Viole (4 sole) con sord.*, with solo cellos and a solo bass pizzicato.
6. **Score 1 caption.** E major is a semitone *below* F, not above. Fixed (“a semitone below F”).
7. **Arts, Ravel letter.** “Lunch or dinner” → lunch; “the morning after” → “the day after” (the letter is dated 26 June and says “hier soir”; the hour is not stated). This ledger’s row 34 is corrected too.
