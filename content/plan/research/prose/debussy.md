### Changed claims and contexts

1. “Nobody in the family played” → “His parents were not musicians”. His uncle Jules-Alexandre is documented as an artist-musician, so the family-wide assertion was false: Lesure/Rolf preview, pp. 3–4, https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781787444515_A42902130/preview-9781787444515_A42902130.pdf
2. Undated Marmontel/Durand reports, including Durand’s excellent pupil/less sketchy and cavalier wording → dated January 1874 Marmontel report and the exact published June 1879 translation “very gifted in harmony, but hopelessly unfocused”. The earlier Durand remark also circulates in reliable programme notes, but the replacement supplies a directly checkable dated report: same Lesure/Rolf preview, pp. 15, 25.
3. Debussy “improvising chords” for Guiraud → “discussing chords” with Guiraud around 1890. Emmanuel’s account has Guiraud playing the example; it does not justify the original action attributed to Debussy: https://bpb-us-e1.wpmucdn.com/sites.tufts.edu/dist/6/2974/files/2015/10/Debussy1.pdf
4. “a week after his return” for Tristan → “Back in Paris that March”, with the Hébert addressee supplied. The documented letter is 17 March 1887, after his 2 March departure from Rome; an exact one-week interval was not established: https://www.qobuz.com/us-en/magazine/story/2017/12/31/debussy-from-achille-to-claude/
5. Sunset quotation left without its original publication context, and old ledger assigning it to Gil Blas → Mercure de France, January 1903: https://fukuoka-u.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/4266/files/A1701_0039.pdf (footnote 32).
6. Gamelan “never developed in the European sense at all” → layers circling in great rhythmic cycles, a positive technical description rather than an unsupported absolute: https://brenthugh.com/debnotes/debussy-gamelan.pdf
7. Palestrina comparison without its periodical context → Revue musicale S.I.M., 1913: https://emf.oicrm.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/1905_Mer_Bourion-VF.pdf
8. Quartet scherzo “plucked almost from end to end” → “plucked for much of its length”, retaining the defining pizzicato without denying substantial bowed passages: https://www.chambermusicsociety.org/about-the-music/composers/claude-debussy
9. Mallarmé at the Faune première / “heard the first night” → at the concert the following day, 23 December 1894, in both life and poem note: https://www.henle.de/media/82/9f/d9/1697725791/1259-1697725791-sync.pdf
10. Golaud’s questions growing worse “until they kill her” → “questions grow more violent”. The plot does not establish that literal cause of Mélisande’s death: https://www.chambermusicsociety.org/about-the-music/composers/claude-debussy ; https://www.classical-music.com/features/composers/claude-debussy
11. Several friends “never spoke to him again” → “several broke with him”. Permanent silence by unspecified people is stronger than the biographical evidence supports: https://open.bu.edu/bitstreams/dff43f1c-f02d-435c-97bc-742faf76d3aa/download
12. Eastbourne sea quotation without addressee/date → letter to Jacques Durand, July 1905: https://www.gutenberg.org/files/56208/56208-h/56208-h.htm ; https://www.bruzanemediabase.com/sites/default/files/2025-11/debussy_lettres_a_son_editeur_0.pdf
13. Lalo’s sea criticism without review date → explicitly 24 October 1905: https://emf.oicrm.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/1905_Mer_Bourion-VF.pdf
14. “Nobody remembered Jeux for forty years” and the repeated forty-year oblivion in lineage prose → documented stage returns in 1920/1923, marginal repertory position, and subsequent importance for Boulez’s generation: https://www.laphil.com/works/jeux ; https://www.gmth.de/zeitschrift/artikel/711.aspx
15. Cancer “diagnosed in 1909” → cancer without an exact diagnosis year. Sources distinguish early symptoms from later diagnosis and disagree on dating; no disputed medical date is asserted: https://www.chambermusicsociety.org/about-the-music/composers/claude-debussy ; https://www.classical-music.com/features/composers/claude-debussy
16. Suite bergamasque’s four pieces all described as old dance shapes, “heavy revision”, and certain derivation of its title from Verlaine → includes minuet/passepied, “after revision”, and title “recalls” Verlaine. Two movements are not dances, the extent of revision is uncertain, and the verbal connection is not proof of title provenance: https://www.henle.de/media/b8/d2/f2/1690886063/0381-1690886063-sync.pdf
17. Schumann/Chopin letter applied to both Images books, with extra words in the quotation → first book, August 1905 letter to Durand, “to the left of Schumann or to the right of Chopin”: https://www.henle.de/media/09/82/90/1690886060/0388-1690886060-sync.pdf
18. Children’s Corner’s six pieces all given English titles → five English titles; Doctor Gradus ad Parnassum is Latin: https://www.henle.de/media/3f/8c/98/1690886064/0382-1690886064-sync.pdf
19. Flute/viola/harp a trio “nobody had tried before” → “an unusual trio”, removing an unestablished universal priority claim: https://brahms4.ircam.fr/fr/work/sonate-en-trio-pour-flute-alto-et-harpe
20. Repeated comparisons caused Ravel’s friendship to sour → comparisons and the cooling in 1904–05 stated separately. Published notes say the cause is not certain: https://www.hyperion-records.co.uk/dc.asp?dc=D_CDA68161
21. 1941 Pelléas “Opéra-Comique forces” and all singers described as raised in that tradition → catalogue credits “Orchestre symphonique, Chœurs Yvonne Gouverné”, with the unestablished blanket background claim removed. Paris, year, Occupation, principal singers and conductor retained: https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb378939719 ; https://www.pristineclassical.com/products/paco063 ; https://www.nypl.org/research/research-catalog/bib/b15593030
22. Jeux “hardly keeps one tempo for more than a few bars” / “the tempo hardly holds still” → frequent changes of pace in its central episodes. Winkler’s score analysis distinguishes the many central rubatos from the relatively constant underlying tempo, so the whole-ballet absolute was misleading: https://www.gmth.de/zeitschrift/artikel/711.aspx
