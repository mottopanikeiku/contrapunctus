### Correction

- *Piano Phase* ‘begins with twelve quick notes played together’ / ‘Begins with twelve notes played together by both pianists’ → one pianist begins alone; the second enters in unison before phasing. Corrected in the life and work description. The composer’s web note abbreviates the initial sequence, but his printed ‘Directions for Performance’ explicitly prescribe the solo first bar and the second player’s fade-in at bar 2: [Universal Edition UE 16156 score scan](https://www.scribd.com/document/725417297/SteveReich-PianoPhase-1957), p. 1 (the upload title’s ‘1957’ is erroneous). [David Feurzeig’s University of Vermont teaching analysis](https://dfeurzei.w3.uvm.edu/210/assignments/asst19.html) independently confirms the sequence. The deliberately reduced alignment diagram is unchanged.

### Quotations confirmed

- ‘Sometimes when people speak, they almost sing’ — Reich to Terry Gross, 1999 interview, excerpt rebroadcast on 12 January 2001; exact words confirmed in the [Fresh Air transcript](https://freshairarchive.org/segments/minimalist-composer-steve-reich). The page’s opening capital is the sole change from the continuous source sentence.
- ‘if I had been in Europe during this period, as a Jew I would have had to ride very different trains’ — exact continuous excerpt from [Reich’s signed 1988 programme note](https://stevereich.com/composition/different-trains/).
- Psalm 19:2–3 — all four lines of the unpointed Hebrew match the [Mechon Mamre Hebrew Bible](https://www.mechon-mamre.org/p/pt/pt2619.htm); the page’s English is a faithful translation. [Reich’s *Tehillim* note](https://stevereich.com/composition/tehillim/) confirms the Hebrew verse numbering and the continuation through verse 5.
