# Journeys — prose ledger

## Fact-check (independent)

Independent source-based check of all six `content/journeys/*.json` (subtitle, intro, captions, station narration, cue text, source titles). No earlier ledger existed. DAHR pages were read from Wayback captures where the live site blocked requests; IMSLP scores were read as page images. No validator-independent claims were taken from the earlier checker.

### Changes

1. `the-recorded-century.json`, s4 narration — “Paderewski dismissed the rule that time borrowed in one phrase must be paid back in another” → “Paderewski rejected, for soloists, the rule …”. Paderewski concedes repayment is natural with an orchestra and rejects it only for soloists. https://polishmusic.usc.edu/research/publications/polish-music-journal/vol4no1/paderewski-tempo-rubato/
2. `the-recorded-century.json`, s5 art caption — “between the pianist’s Edison and later Victor recordings” → “six years after the pianist’s Edison session”. Rachmaninoff’s Victor sessions began in 1920 (DAHR B-24123 lists 17 May 1920) and the Victor Prelude was made in October 1921, so a 1925 portrait is not between the two. Edison 1919: https://web.archive.org/web/20071108225217/http://www.nps.gov/archive/edis/edisonia/classical.htm ; DAHR: https://adp.library.ucsb.edu/index.php/matrix/detail/700009327/B-24123-Lilacs_op._21_no._5
3. `vienna-1900.json`, s2 — “At the opening on 14 April, Mahler conducted …” → “At the preview on 14 April, the day before the exhibition opened to the public, Mahler conducted …”. The Secession gives 15 April as the first day (https://secession.at/beethovenfrieze); the 14 April performance is the preview (https://mahlerfoundation.org/mahler/locations/austria/vienna/secession-exhibitions/ ; https://www.classical-music.com/articles/beethoven-frieze).
4. `vienna-1900.json`, s4 — Urlicht’s first phrase “is surrounded by solemn brass chords” → “is answered by solemn brass chords” (brass/trumpet chorale follows the first words; matches the cue). https://mahlerfoundation.org/mahler/listening-guide/listening-guide-movement-4-urlicht-sehr-feierlich-aber-schlicht/ ; https://mahlerfoundation.org/mahler/compositions/symphony-no-2/symphony-no-2-movement-4-lied-1-urlicht-sehr-feierlich-aber-schlicht/
5. `vienna-1900.json`, s6 — “trees and paths become strange” → “the trees and paths the speaker loved grow so pale that he hardly knows them” (George: “bäum und wege die ich liebte fahlen / Dass ich sie kaum mehr kenne”). https://archive2.schoenberg.at/compositions/werke_einzelansicht.php?werke_id=404&herkunft=allewerke
6. `rachmaninoff-in-exile.json`, s3 — “wrote Lilacs in the summer of 1902” → “in 1902” (sources give April 1902 at Ivanovka, e.g. Carl Fischer editor’s note https://www.stantons.com/media/488734/cfn-CM9376.pdf, against “summer” in https://www.yevgenysudbin.com/artist.php?view=prog&rid=458); “In 1913 he made the song into a piano piece … A decade later, on 27 December 1923” → “Later he made the song into a piano piece … On 27 December 1923 he recorded that version”. The transcription is dated 1913 (Sudbin), 1914 (Hyperion CDH55458 notes, https://www.hyperion-records.co.uk/dc.asp?dc=D_CDH55458) and 1915 elsewhere; the year is left out.
7. `rachmaninoff-in-exile.json`, s9 source title — “Lilacs, 1902 song and 1913 transcription” → “Lilacs, the 1902 song and its piano transcription” (same reason).
