## Editorial decisions and limits

- Corrected the Ligeti meeting to September 1945 using the more precise EMB chronology; retained 1946 for the start of Academy studies.
- Removed the unsupported causal claim that performing on a small upright discouraged concert-grand spectacle. The documented upright in *Stele* is a separate scoring fact.
- Replaced general assertions about every fragment’s supposedly specified final silence with named musical cases, especially the interrupted end of *Officium breve*.
- Did not repeat the exact 1812 letter/transmission date for the Hölderlin poem. The image, original poem and existing metadata remain untouched.
- Did not use the search summary that erroneously assigned Ligeti’s title *Métamorphoses nocturnes* to Kurtág’s Op. 1; the actual publisher and recording sources were used instead.
- The centenary is supported by a post-event eyewitness account, not just an advance announcement. `died: null` remains protected and unchanged.
- Critical images such as the musical address book, the semitone’s pressure, the disappearance of a phrase, and Bach’s long breaths are editorial readings [INFERENCE]. They do not introduce biographical events. No unresolved factual prerequisite remains for the authored text.

### Corrections and clarification

- *An Zimmern* date: “Before 1812” → “1812”, following the [Bibliotheca Augustana transcription of Beißner’s 1953 edition](https://www.tha.de/~harsch/germanica/Chronologie/19Jh/Hoelderlin/hoe_1201.html) and the [Hölderlinturm museum’s account and reproduction of Zimmer’s 1812 letter](https://hoelderlinturm.de/sonderausstellungen/hoelderlin-liebt/turm/). The parent checked the renderer and confirmed that this arts date is not a protected timeline field; the original poem text is unchanged.
- Transmission note: “Zimmer’s copy preserved these lines” → “Zimmer preserved these lines in an 1812 letter to the poet’s mother”. This identifies the actual document and recipient from the [museum’s primary-document presentation](https://hoelderlinturm.de/sonderausstellungen/hoelderlin-liebt/turm/), replacing the earlier ledger’s Wikipedia-dependent provenance. The household’s 1807 start is separately supported by the [Hölderlin-Gesellschaft chronology](https://www.hoelderlin-gesellschaft.de/website/de/friedrich-hoelderlin/leben); the museum’s accessible page says 1806 in its opening summary, so it was not used for that date.
