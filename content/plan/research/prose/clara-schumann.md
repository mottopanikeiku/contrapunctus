The two 1853 diary quotations use the English wording printed by Stephen Rodgers, who cites Grace Hadow’s translation of Litzmann; the 1868 quotation is translated from the German printed by Schumann-Portal. Recording recommendations are editorial judgements informed by the reviews below, not a claim of a new listening session.

## Corrections and exclusions

- Corrected the first Op. 11 Romance’s key from E♭ major to E♭ minor.
- Extended Op. 21 to 1853–55: the published first movement replaced the original 1853 piece.
- Replaced the uncertain 1841 composition date for the second Scherzo with the documented ‘Published 1845’.
- Avoided treating Clara as either an exclusively domestic composer or a woman who stopped composing completely at Robert’s death.
- Identity fields, image filenames, ABC, poem original and translation, reading entries and lineage arrays remain unchanged. Removed `featured`.

### Corrections

- [EXT] Two marriages sixty-one years apart → two settings sixty-one years apart, both gifts between spouses. Clara’s marriage was in 1840, while her Rückert manuscript gift is dated 8 June 1841. The interval in the original sentence was between musical settings, not wedding dates: [Schumann chronology][C1], [Clara’s presentation manuscript][C8], [Mahler’s 1902 autograph and provenance][C22]. The corrected sentence now identifies the objects actually being compared.
- [EXT] Forty further years of organising tours after Robert’s 1856 death → decades afterwards. The original duration ran past her last English tour in 1888 and last public concert in 1891: [chronology][C1], [biographical introduction][C2]. The separate epithet’s forty-year interval, from the 1828 début to the 1868 letter, remains correct.
- [INFERENCE] Removed the imperative inviting the reader to listen for the fugue’s successive entries; retained the same factual account as a statement. The [scholarly chamber notes][C9] support the fugal finale. No other stylistic rewriting undertaken.
