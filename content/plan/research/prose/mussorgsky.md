## Editorial decisions

- Removed `featured`.
- The parallel-chord ABC remains an explicitly invented schematic. The caption no longer uses it as evidence for a particular chord progression in Mussorgsky or a direct borrowing by Debussy.
- The hospital portrait is not treated as an adequate summary of the whole man; the opening also restores Borodin’s account of the sociable young officer.
- Existing dates, titles, image identities and reading references are retained. General judgements about rawness, authenticity or the superiority of one editor have been replaced by identifiable versions and audible details.

### Quotations confirmed

- “Sounds and ideas hung in the air”: letter to Stasov, probably 12 June 1874 (“Hartmann is boiling as Boris boiled—sounds and ideas hung in the air…”); https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pictures_at_an_Exhibition
- “If only I did not have to go to work as well… that’s what stands in the way”: letter to Shestakova, 2 August 1876 (full: “Almost everything is composed; now one has to write and rewrite…”); Orlova note 45, IU Press URL above.
- Borodin’s 1856 reminiscence (close-fitting uniform, smoothed hair, well-groomed hands, extracts from *Trovatore* and *Traviata*, admirers around the piano) matches the opening paragraph; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modest_Mussorgsky (October 1856, hospital).
- Nadezhda Purgold’s diary, 30 April 1870 (the “kids” written for Sasha, “the only one who could perform them as they should be performed”): Orlova note 4 to N. Rimskaia-Korsakova, IU Press URL above.

### Facts confirmed (one line each)

- Repin sat 2–5 March 1881 (OS), four sittings: Orlova, Repin note 10 (IU Press URL). Admitted to the hospital 12 February 1881 (OS): Leonova note 8. Leonova tour summer 1879, southern Russia then principal cities: Stasov note 52 and Leonova notes.
- Birth 21 March 1839 / death 28 March 1881, a week after his 42nd birthday; Karevo; Gerke lessons; 1852 polka at his father’s expense; cadet school; 1856 Preobrazhensky commission; 1858 resignation; 1863 civil service and six-man commune; serf emancipation cost the family half its estate: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modest_Mussorgsky
- Dedication of *The Nursery* no. 1 “With Nanny” to Dargomyzhsky, “great teacher of musical truth”: https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Детская_(Мусоргский) (via search extract; not opened separately) and https://www.belcanto.ru/or-mussorgsky-detskaya.html (search extract); *The Nursery* 1868–72, own texts: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Nursery
- *The Orphan* is a beggar’s plea (“Have pity on this poor creature, this wretched, homeless orphan!”): Brilliant Classics song texts, https://www.brilliantclassics.com/media/551357/94928-Mussorgsky-Songs-Sung-Texts-download.pdf ; *The Seminarist* (1866), *Savishna* (1866): same source.
- *Boris*: 1869 version, seven scenes, no leading female role, rejected 10 February 1871; 1872 version adds Marina, the two Polish scenes and the Kromy scene, drops St Basil’s; 1874 première (27 January OS) with the Cell scene cut; rooms shared with Rimsky-Korsakov during the revision year: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Godunov_(opera) ; seven-scene count also in a programme note, https://sheetmusicinternational.com/program-notes/mussorgsky-modest-1839-1881-boris-godunov-opera-791 (via search extract).
- *Khovanshchina*: vocal score, unfinished ending; Rimsky 1881–82, Stravinsky–Ravel 1913, Shostakovich 1958: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khovanshchina . *Bare Mountain* completed 23 June 1867, refused by Balakirev, never performed in his lifetime: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Night_on_Bald_Mountain . *Salammbô* material reused in *Boris*: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salammb%C3%B4_(Mussorgsky)
- Hartmann died 4 August 1873 aged 39; Promenade 5/4 then 6/4, bars 1–2 right hand alone, pitches and rhythm identical to the ABC; dedication to Stasov: Mutopia source https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/MussorgskyM/pictures-at-an-exhibition/pictures-at-an-exhibition-lys/promenade-1.ly
- Trilby (Gerber, Petipa, 1871), child dancers, canary chicks in eggs; Old Castle troubadour, alto saxophone in Ravel (1922): BSO note above. Commons record for the chicks sketch (1871, Pushkin House): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hartmann_Chicks_sketch_for_Trilby_ballet.jpg
- Songs and Dances of Death 1875–77, Shostakovich orchestration for Vishnevskaya, 1962, after his own *Boris* and *Khovanshchina*: https://www.boosey.com/cr/music/Modest-Moussorgsky-Songs-and-Dances-of-Death/5311
- Richter, Sofia 1958: thin sound, audience coughing: https://www.theguardian.com/culture/2001/feb/09/classicalmusicandopera . Reiner/Chicago 1957: first trumpet’s rapid precise staccato in *Schmuyle*, and *Great Gate* kept moving: https://musicwebinternational.com/2025/01/mussorgsky-pictures-at-an-exhibition-gramola/ . Abbado/Berlin 1993: Kotcherga (spelt Kocherga there) “lyrical, inward reading… pays dividends in his death scene”, Mussorgsky’s second version plus the first scene of Act IV from 1869: https://www.musicweb-international.com/classrev/2018/Apr/Mussorgsky_Boris_19075811172.htm
- Debussy’s admiration for Mussorgsky’s songs (*Monsieur Croche* essay on *The Nursery*): documented in secondary extracts only (search results, https://ivopogorelich.com/mussorgsky-ravel/ , not opened); no figure or date from it is used on the page.
