### Correction

- “entries a fifth apart” → “entries climbing and descending in fifths”. The fugue does not proceed in a single sequence of successive fifths: alternating entries extend upward and downward around the circle from A, meeting at E♭. Houston Symphony explicitly describes the alternating scheme; Hartmut Schick describes the double circular fugue and tritone climax. https://houstonsymphony.org/bartok-music-strings-percussion-celesta/ ; https://epub.ub.uni-muenchen.de/92268/1/Schick_Folklorism_Symmetry_Tritone.pdf (p. 144).
