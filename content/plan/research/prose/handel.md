## Editorial limits

The portrait identification, image files, poem original, reading list and lineage arrays are preserved. Neither schematic ABC string is changed. No doubtful recollection is used as direct speech: the spoken voices come from dated correspondence and Handel’s dated score annotation. The biographical narrative keeps the opera business, royal employment, charitable performances and late oratorios in view.
