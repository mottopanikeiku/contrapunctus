- Replaced the broad Nancarrow-as-model assertion with the documented two-piano arrangements. No lineage entry was added or removed.
- Omitted the original general claim that later scenes in *The Tempest* share particular harmonic processes: the checked analysis supports the concrete interval-cycle account, which is retained.
- Removed the unsupported assertion that *Dante*’s sections require different “organisations of orchestral time”; supplied documented instruments, voices and collaborators instead.

### Correction

- **Piano Concerto finale, two occurrences:** the life’s “a three-chord call launches a tumbling theme that Adès likens to a ball bouncing downstairs” and the work note’s equivalent identification → a three-chord call launches the opening tumbling theme; **later the piano introduces another theme**, which Adès compares to a ball bouncing downstairs. His own programme note explicitly says “Eventually the piano takes up a new theme in the style of a ball bouncing down stairs”. The two themes must not be conflated. The matching earlier ledger row is corrected too. Source: https://www.fabermusic.com/music/concerto-for-piano-and-orchestra.
