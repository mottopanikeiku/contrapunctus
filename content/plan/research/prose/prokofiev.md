## Claims removed or corrected

- Removed the unspecific claim that Prokofiev and Shostakovich “learnt from each other”: the consulted sources support Shostakovich learning from Prokofiev, but did not establish the reciprocal mechanism asserted by the old note. Protected lineage arrays are unchanged.
- Corrected the interlocking-hands schematic caption from quavers to semiquavers; the ABC was left untouched.
- Corrected Dicksee’s pairing from the first balcony encounter/Act I to the later farewell/Act III.
- Removed the blanket “complete” claim for the Gergiev opera recording; the recommendation identifies its singers and theatrical strengths without asserting a definitive textual edition.
- Compressed repeated formal explanations and the long chronology to the required life range. Existing work, image and reading entries were retained. Unused disputed details (the exact cause of the Second Concerto manuscript’s loss, the motive for the Seventh’s added ending) are not asserted.
