## Editorial decisions and removals

- Removed `featured` as requested. Identity, dates and era, portrait file/focus, all image filenames, both ABC strings, the French poem and the reading array are retained; lineage endpoints are unchanged.
- Replaced the unsupported generalisation about the influence of Berg’s “coded intimacy” on Kurtág with the documented Britten study proposal and Boulez’s 1979 production. Kurtág remains in the protected lineage array.
- Dropped the general claim about appoggiaturas retaining expressive weight without a tonic; the voice paragraph now describes the Captain’s falsetto, Wozzeck’s repeated note and Marie’s lullaby.
- Avoided attributing discovery of the hidden Baudelaire vocal setting solely to Perle. The text identifies his examination of Hanna’s annotated copy and describes the hidden text separately; Douglass Green’s role is not erased by an exclusive discovery claim.
- The whole *Lyric Suite* is not called twelve-tone. Sources sometimes overstate this; the page describes specific ciphers, borrowed music and the movement plan.
- The concert rows and chorale pitches are explained as schematics. No new bar-exact quotation is asserted for the protected ABC.
- Biography source disagreements about the concerto’s precise August completion date and the publisher’s erroneous cause of Manon’s death were resolved by using “August” and the well-attested diagnosis of polio.

No builds, tests, formatters, git commands or automated validation were run. Integration validation belongs to the parent.
