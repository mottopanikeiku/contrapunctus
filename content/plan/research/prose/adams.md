## Removed or qualified

- Replaced the old Chamber Symphony composition date with its documented 1993 première date.
- Corrected the incomplete *Antony and Cleopatra* libretto-consultation credit by adding Lucia Scheckner.
- Kept the portrait date explicitly as an upload year; no inferred date of exposure.
- No unattested aphorism has been added as a stand-alone quotation. The often attributed “minimalist bored with minimalism” is not used: Adams’s own Gates note says the phrase was another writer’s.

### Source cautions

The Gates page reverses durations in its footer; its narrative explicitly identifies Phrygian Gates as twenty-two minutes. The Earbox Klinghoffer narrative misdates the hijacking to 1984; Ross independently supplies 1985. The MusicWeb review misdates the septet to 1977; Adams supplies 1978. None of those source errors has entered the composer page. Protected notation, images, poems, dates, reading and lineage arrays were not altered.
