## Rights: recording and composition are separate

The selected commercial phonograms use the existing `eu-published-before-1963` project basis, with publication years 1961 and 1959 respectively. INFERENCE: under that rule their former 50-year phonogram terms had expired before the 2013 extension. [Directive 2011/77/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX%3A32011L0077), recitals 1–3 and Article 1(2)–(3), was read for the term and transition. This is territorial phonogram treatment, not US or worldwide clearance and not a CC licence.

**None of the three hosted recordings is presented as a US-government recording. Copland’s compositions are not presented as public domain.** [The Fund’s permissions page](https://www.aaroncopland.com/pages/permissions-and-rights/) names Boosey & Hawkes, a Concord company, as exclusive agent for musical-work permissions. The Fanfare analysis below explicitly warns that the piece is not public domain. [Boosey’s Orchestral Variations catalogue](https://www.boosey.com/cr/perusals/score?id=1043) lists worldwide territory. Composition permission is not established by a historical phonogram date; every dossier preserves this distinction.

For the government-recording investigation, [17 USC §105](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title17/html/USCODE-2024-title17-chap1-sec105.htm) and its explanatory notes were read. Official-duty federal work may have a US-government recording basis, but government use of a privately written composition does not remove its copyright. Third-party narration and production also require their own assessment.

The article names its score reference as *Boosey & Hawkes Orchestral Anthology*, vol. 1, pp. 131–134. The opening percussion-before-trumpet statement and the broad, open 5–1–5 trumpet spacing are supported for the composer author to reuse.

```sh
node tools/audio.mjs analyze content/listen/copland-fanfare-common-man.mjs content/listen/copland-orchestral-variations.mjs content/listen/copland-lincoln-portrait.mjs
```

Observed output:

This downloaded and decoded the actual selected streams and generated the three `docs/assets/listen/<id>.json` files. No aural review or score alignment is claimed. Every cue uses `precision: 'passage'`; none claims a moment-exact event. Musical boundaries are INFERENCE, supported by documented form and the selected recording’s features:

## Government recordings investigated

Actual discovery commands included `node tools/audio.mjs search Copland`, plus work-only searches for `Fanfare Common Man`, `Shaker Melody` and `Lincoln Portrait`; and `archive` searches for each work. No qualifying Commons recording of these pieces was found. The broad Copland result included a MIDI chord file, which was rejected. Searching only the work names did not cure the gap.

- **Marine Band Fanfare:** [Archive’s PatrioticMedleyFeaturingTheMilitaryBands metadata](https://archive.org/metadata/PatrioticMedleyFeaturingTheMilitaryBands/metadata) explicitly attributes `100010429.mp3` to the Library of Congress Copland Centennial concert. `archive-files PatrioticMedleyFeaturingTheMilitaryBands` lists a 212.49-second MP3 but no item licence. [LOC item JSON](https://www.loc.gov/item/ihas.100010429/?fo=json) documents the US Marine Band, 14 November 2000, Coolidge Auditorium. It also says ©1944, copyright renewed, used by permission of Boosey & Hawkes, and marks the resource `download_restricted: true`. A legacy nested field says “No known restrictions”; the explicit copyright/permission notice was not overridden by that general field. The mirror was not selected.
- **Another Marine Band Fanfare:** the Fund links [this performance](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0KxMc_tyQBo). Its description identifies television episode 4436, aired 21 September 2014; [oEmbed](https://www.youtube.com/oembed?url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0KxMc_tyQBo&format=json) identifies the uploader as **The Tabernacle Choir at Temple Square**, not the Marine Band. No government-production right was assumed from the performer’s name.
- **Shaker variations:** the [Army Field Band/Hamilton official track](https://music.apple.com/us/song/variations-on-a-shaker-melody-variations-on-a-shaker-melody/389419562) is available through Altissimo!’s *The Legacy of Aaron Copland: Emblems*. The [album page](https://music.apple.com/us/album/the-legacy-of-aaron-copland-emblems/389419443) gives 3:32 and the Altissimo! imprint. An [Archive “Variations on Copland” upload](https://archive.org/metadata/variations-on-a-shaker-melody-arr.-b.-lerner/metadata) supplies neither a usable rights statement nor a reliable performer chain; rejected. Commons’ Shaker-name results include unrelated Simple Gifts arrangements, not a securely matched Copland recording.
- **Lincoln Portrait:** [Marine Band video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RrMHbSk7IUQ), official [oEmbed attribution](https://www.youtube.com/oembed?url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RrMHbSk7IUQ&format=json): Jason K. Fettig, Jim Lehrer, 23 February 2015, Music Center at Strathmore, North Bethesda. This is an official complete performance, but no qualifying downloadable Commons/IA path or independent narration/production clearance was established. The Army Field Band album above also lists Charles Osgood in Lincoln Portrait (15:57). Neither is confused with the hosted Sandburg/Kostelanetz recording.

The direct Marine Band website and several historical magazine PDFs returned HTTP 403. A substitute 1961 magazine PDF had no extracted text; no uninspected magazine review is used as publication evidence. The successful primary carrier/catalogue evidence is specified above.

## Elsewhere items and their facts

1. [Sony’s Bernstein Century Copland collection on Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/bernstein-century-copland-appalachian-spring-rodeo/201044682): Leonard Bernstein/New York Philharmonic; full track list for Appalachian Spring, four Rodeo episodes and Billy the Kid suite. The composition range 1939–45 is checked against [Billy the Kid suite](https://www.aaroncopland.com/works/billy-the-kid-suite/), [Rodeo episodes](https://www.aaroncopland.com/works/four-dance-episodes-from-rodeo/) and [Appalachian orchestral suite](https://www.aaroncopland.com/works/appalachian-spring-suite-for-full-orchestra/). The frontier street and corral scenes are their named sections; Simple Gifts is the tune identified by the Shaker-variation catalogue. This is suite coverage, not a claim to contain the original complete ballets.
2. [Army Field Band/Hamilton Shaker track](https://music.apple.com/us/song/variations-on-a-shaker-melody-variations-on-a-shaker-melody/389419562): official performing credits and Altissimo! album chain. [Fund work catalogue](https://www.aaroncopland.com/works/variations-on-a-shaker-melody-from-appalachian-spring-arr-band/) establishes the 1956 symphonic-band version, Simple Gifts alternate title, extraction from Appalachian Spring, saxophones and euphonium. Apple’s track date and album reissue date differ; neither is treated as the session date.

Jennifer DeLapp-Birkett’s [Appalachian Spring audiophile guide](https://www.aaroncopland.com/news/midday-thoughts-which-appalachian-spring-an-audiophiles-guide/) was read in raw form: it identifies Bernstein’s New York Philharmonic recording with the 1945 orchestral suite, and distinguishes original ballet, extended suite and chamber suite. No uploaded video is needed for either published elsewhere item; the two links are official catalogue streams. Ballet and Shaker items add works/versions absent from the three Hall tracks without duplicating another Fanfare or Lincoln recording.
