# Rameau — quotation and fact sources

English quotations are our translations of the French passages identified below. Both original ABC schematics and the original Racine excerpt are preserved. Sources were searched for and read during the rewrite; subjective characterisations of music and recordings are editorial judgements.

## Corrections and exclusions

- No unsupported personal anecdote or invented dialogue was added. The two Rameau quotations are identified by work or recipient and date in the life.
- The reason *Les Boréades* was abandoned is left unstated; conflicting retrospective explanations were not promoted to fact.
- The approximate date of the third keyboard collection is preserved. Protected image filenames, Racine’s original, ABC, reading list and lineage arrays are unchanged.

### Confirmed (source, one line each)

- First harpsichord book, Paris 1706, composer “à vingt-trois ans”; Clermont 1703, Dijon, Lyon 1713–15, Clermont again, Paris at the end of 1722 or early 1723; Traité 1722; third book c. 1728; 1727 request to La Motte; *Paladins* 1760; Querelle des Bouffons 1752; Cabinet appointment 1745; *Boréades* never performed in his lifetime: [Larousse](https://www.larousse.fr/encyclopedie/personnage/Jean-Philippe_Rameau/140266).
- 1706 suite in A minor: unmeasured prelude then a fast section, two allemandes, courante, gigue, two sarabandes; “twenty-three-year-old”: [Esfahani, Hyperion CDA68071/2](https://www.hyperion-records.co.uk/dc.asp?dc=D_CDA68071%2F2). Same note: *Les Cyclopes* preface (drumstick hands; left hand crossing over), *Le Rappel des oiseaux* (1724), *La Poule*, *L’Enharmonique* explained in the preface, Gavotte with six doubles. His dating of the third book (c. 1729–30) and “Huron” dancers conflict with the Mercure/BnF accounts below and are not adopted.
- Letter to La Motte, 25 October 1727: “… vous verrez pour lors, que je ne suis pas novice dans l’art … où je tâche de cacher l’art par l’art même”; he points to two cantatas (*L’Enlèvement d’Orithie*, *Thétis*), to the harpsichord pieces (*Les Sauvages* “qui parurent sur le Théâtre Italien il y a un ou deux ans”, *Les Cyclopes*, *Les Tourbillons* …) and to motets; copied “sur l’original trouvé parmi les papiers de M. de la Motte”: [full text, Rameau 2014 / CMBV](http://rameau2014.fr/RESSOURCES/Catalogue/Correspondance/LETTRE-DE-M.-RAMEAU-A-M.-HOUDART-DE-LA-MOTTE-25-octobre-1727); printed posthumously in the *Mercure de France*, March 1765, pp. 36–40 ([DICteCo](https://dicteco.huma-num.fr/document/63642)).
- Two Indians dancing at the Comédie-Italienne in 1725 and *Les Sauvages* composed some months later; 1735 entrées (Incas, Turc généreux, Fleurs), 1736 addition; Zima, Damon, Don Alvar: [BnF](https://heritage.bnf.fr/france-ameriques/sauvages-damerique-dans-indes-galantes). The dancers’ Louisiana origin is in the *Mercure* of September 1725 (“venus depuis peu de la Louisiane”), seen as a search extract of a transcription (jp.rameau.free.fr, not opened).
- La Pouplinière as patron; Pellegrin and Voltaire met there; Compositeur du Cabinet 1745: [Paris Opéra biography](https://www.operadeparis.fr/artistes/jean-philippe-rameau).
- *Hippolyte*: Pellegrin’s Racine adaptation; second act in the Underworld; Act III rigaudons while Thésée suppresses his rage; hunt and horns, flutes as wind in the sea-monster scene; première 1 October 1733: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hippolyte_et_Aricie); [Larousse](https://www.larousse.fr/encyclopedie/personnage/Jean-Philippe_Rameau/140266). Pellegrin’s preface: “Les Parques annoncent à Thesée … qu’il retrouvera ces mêmes Enfers chez lui” (CMBV dossier).
- *Castor et Pollux*: 1737 opens with the funeral, *Tristes apprêts* with bassoon obbligato, 1754 prologue cut and new first act; Berlioz; Schola Cantorum 1903 with Debussy present: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castor_et_Pollux).
- *Platée* for the Dauphin’s wedding, haute-contre title role: [Paris Opéra](https://www.operadeparis.fr/saison-15-16/opera/platee).
- *Boréades*: rehearsed 1763, plot, first full staging Aix, July 1982, Gardiner: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Bor%C3%A9ades).
- Recordings: Christie *Castor* 1737, Crook/Mellon/Gens, Harmonia Mundi, recorded September 1992: [Opéra Baroque](https://operabaroque.fr/CASTOR_1.htm). Minkowski *Platée*, Ragon, Jennifer Smith, Erato, recorded December 1988: [Opéra Baroque](https://operabaroque.fr/PLATEE_1.htm). Gardiner *Boréades*, Erato, recorded July 1982 at Aix at the world stage première: [Opéra Baroque](https://operabaroque.fr/BOREADES_2.htm). Rousset, Oiseau-Lyre 425 886-2 (1989): [harpsichord discography](https://www.medieval.org/emfaq/velde/harps.htm).
- Portrait attribution (Aved, formerly Chardin), c. 1728, Dijon: [Commons record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jean-Philippe_Rameau_by_Jacques_Aved.jpg).

### Not verified

- The Racine verses (protected data) were not re-checked against a second edition in this pass; they match the standard text of *Phèdre*, I, 3.
- *Pièces de clavecin en concerts* instrumentation (violin or flute, viol or second violin) rests on the existing ledger line; Larousse confirms five concerts for two melodic instruments and harpsichord.
