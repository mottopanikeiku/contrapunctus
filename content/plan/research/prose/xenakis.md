# Xenakis — quotation and fact sources

Research for the new composer chapter (`content/composers/xenakis.mjs`) and its data files. Sources were opened on 19 October 2026. Quotations are limited to short fragments; each is attributed in the page. Translations from French are mine. Wikipedia was used only as a pointer to coordinates. No hosted recordings are offered: every recording of Xenakis’s music is either after 1962 or in copyright, so the music is carried by `content/elsewhere/xenakis.json`.

Short names used below:

- **Chronology** — [Les Amis de Xenakis, Biography, chronology by Anne-Sylvie Barthel-Calvet](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/en/biographie/); French text at [iannis-xenakis.org/biographie](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/biographie/).
- **CIX** — [Constellation Iannis Xenakis, Biographical Elements](https://www.centre-iannis-xenakis.org/iannis_xenakis_bio?lang=en).
- **Varga** — Bálint András Varga, *Conversations with Iannis Xenakis* (Faber and Faber, 1996), the [copy hosted by Les Amis de Xenakis](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/96-Varga-B.A.-Conversations-with-Iannis-Xenakis.pdf). Interviews of 1980 and 1989. Page numbers are the book’s own.
- **Pimienta** — Guy Pimienta (trans. Sharon Kanach), “Iannis Xenakis/Le Corbusier: A Confrontation en sol dur”, chapter 22 of Kanach and Nelson, eds., [*Meta-Xenakis*](https://books.openbookpublishers.com/10.11647/obp.0390/ch22.xhtml) (Open Book Publishers, 2024; CC BY-NC 4.0).
- **Christensen–Bjerno** — Erik Christensen and Lise C. Bjerno, “Iannis Xenakis’s *Pithoprakta*: A Phenomenological Approach”, [*Meta-Xenakis*, chapter 18](https://books.openbookpublishers.com/10.11647/obp.0390/ch18.xhtml).
- **Turner** — Charles Turner, [*Xenakis in America*](https://monoskop.org/images/6/63/Turner_Charles_Xenakis_in_America.pdf) (One Block Avenue, 2014; revision of a CUNY dissertation, 2014).
- **Dictionary** — Dimitris Exarchos, ed., [*A Xenakis Dictionary*](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/en/dictionary/) (Les Amis de Xenakis, 2023): entries opened were [Ruled Surfaces](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/en/ruled-surfaces/) (Kiourtsoglou), [Undulating Glass Panes](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/en/undulating-glass-panes/) (Kiourtsoglou), [Stochastic Music](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/en/stochastic-music/) and [Group Theory](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/en/group-theory/) (Gibson), [Sieve Theory](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/en/sieve-theory/) (Exarchos), [UPIC](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/en/dictionary-upic/) (Di Scipio), [Polytope](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/en/polytope/) (Carré), [Arborescence](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/en/arborescence/) (Harley).
- **Work records** — Les Amis de Xenakis work pages: [Metastaseis](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/en/metastaseis/), [Pithoprakta](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/en/pithoprakta/), [Achorripsis](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/en/achorripsis/), [Concret PH](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/en/concret-ph/), [Herma](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/en/herma/), [Eonta](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/en/eonta/), [Nomos alpha](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/en/nomos-alpha/), [Terretektorh](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/en/terretektorh/), [Nuits](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/en/nuits/), [Persephassa](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/en/persephassa/), [Psappha](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/en/psappha/), [Jonchaies](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/en/jonchaies/), [O-Mega](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/en/o-mega/), [Pavillon Philips](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/en/pavillon-philips-bruxelles/), [Polytope de Mycènes](https://www.iannis-xenakis.org/en/polytope-de-mycenes/).
