Each `<id>.json` compares two passages in the same recording: `id` matches the filename, `track` selects `docs/assets/data/<track>/score.json`, and `title`, `question` (at most 12 words), `intro` (at most 60 words) and the one-sentence `listenFor` supply the reader-facing text; `kind` is `literal repeat`, `varied return` or `tonal return`, and `status` is `provisional`. `first` and `second` each contain a `label` and inclusive `from`/`to` locators `{ "n": "1", "occ": 1 }`; traverse the score’s performed `bars` array, not a numeric range or a fixed occurrence, so Satie’s return runs through 1–31 occurrence 2 and 40–47 occurrence 1. Without `pairs`, equal-length passages pair one-to-one in that order; otherwise `pairs` explicitly lists every bar once on each side in performed order as `[aN, aOcc, bN, bOcc]`, using `[aN, aOcc, null, null]` for a first-side-only bar and `[null, null, bN, bOcc]` for a second-side-only bar. `scoreChanges` contains score-derived differences only, each with starting locators `a` and `b` as `[n, occ]` or `null` and plain-English `text`; the control has an empty array. An addition inside an existing bar can have `a: null` without making that whole bar unpaired: Beethoven’s oboe cadenza expands bar 268, paired with bar 21, rather than adding numbered bars. The recapitulation comparison stops at 373, before the coda; its rewritten buildup’s editorial pairing is explained in `scoreChanges`. `sources` lists `{ "title", "url" }` citations; orchestral pitch names in the prose are sounding pitches, while spellings follow the written parts. The build, not these files, computes bar-duration differences and relative level within this recording; unmatched bars have no two-sided difference, and a longer written passage or cadenza is not evidence of a slower tempo. Timing is aligned automatically and has not been checked by ear: `tools/align/REPORT.md` records particular uncertainty in Beethoven’s quiet passages, cadenza and fermata releases, and in Schubert’s quiet close. Keep those provisional measurements separate from written differences and never attribute them to performers’ intentions.
