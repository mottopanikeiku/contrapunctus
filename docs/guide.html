Tour controls

Next and Back, or the arrow keys, move between steps; ‘Do this step’ operates the highlighted control. Skip or Escape closes the tour; playback continues. The current step is kept if the page is reloaded.

Saved pages are available offline; recordings stream over an internet connection. On a later visit the player loads the last recording and waits for Play.

𝄞Contrapunctus

An atlas of composers and their music. Dates follow the standard biographies; catalogue numbers follow the modern thematic catalogues (BWV, K., D., Op.). Where a story is legend and not documented, the text says so.
