At your own pace

Use Next and Back, or the arrow keys. ‘Do it for me’ operates the highlighted control. Skip or Escape closes the tour without stopping your recording. Refreshing a page restores your place in an unfinished tour.

Pages you have visited can be read offline once cached. Audio streams need a connection; no recordings are downloaded for offline listening. On a later visit, your last recording returns paused.

𝄞Contrapunctus

A listener’s atlas built for reading slowly. Dates follow the standard biographies; catalogue numbers follow the modern thematic catalogues (BWV, K., D., Op.). Where a story is legend rather than record, the text says so.
