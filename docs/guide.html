At your own pace

Move with Next and Back or the arrow keys; ‘Do it for me’ uses the highlighted control. Skip or Escape closes the tour and leaves the music playing. Your place in the tour survives a page refresh.

Saved pages are available offline; recordings stream over an internet connection. On your next visit, the player remembers your last recording and waits for you to press Play.

𝄞Contrapunctus

A listener’s atlas built for reading slowly. Dates follow the standard biographies; catalogue numbers follow the modern thematic catalogues (BWV, K., D., Op.). Where a story is legend rather than record, the text says so.
