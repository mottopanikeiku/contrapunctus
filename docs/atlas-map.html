Key, sources and method

Gold dots mark places; grouped portraits open a menu of composers.

Each year’s location favours a dated post over a home or other stay, with the latest record breaking ties. Between dated stays, the last place remains until the next residence or death, marked by a dashed portrait frame.

City cards distinguish residence-based locations from dated events; sharing a city and year is not proof of a meeting.

Selecting a composer shows every recorded place, with overseas stops on a separate inset.

Lines join records in date order; dotted lines mark musical connections.

The Mercator map uses Natural Earth 1:50m, public domain, from the pinned source revision; outlines show present-day countries.

Download the base map · Download dates and overlaps. Each composer’s card gives the full records and sources.

An atlas of composers and their music. Dates follow the standard biographies; catalogue numbers follow the modern thematic catalogues (BWV, K., D., Op.). Where a story is legend and not documented, the text says so.
