Two kinds of evidence

Keep the score and the sound apart

Read the written change

Pitch, key, instrumentation, register and written length come from the cited score. A literal repeat supplies a control: the notation stays the same.

Measure the recorded return

Bar durations and relative levels come from this recording. A longer pause or an added solo can lengthen a bar without establishing a change of tempo or an expressive intention.
