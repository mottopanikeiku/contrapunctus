Method

Score and performance

The score

The changed notes, keys and instruments are listed under Written changes. In a literal repeat, the score stays the same.

The performance

The charts give the time each bar takes and how its volume changes. Pauses and added notes count towards a bar’s length.

𝄞Contrapunctus

An atlas of composers and their music. Dates follow the standard biographies; catalogue numbers follow the modern thematic catalogues (BWV, K., D., Op.). Where a story is legend and not documented, the text says so.
