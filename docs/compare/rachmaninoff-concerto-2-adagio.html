The comparison

Muted strings make the short modulation from C minor to E major, and the piano enters quietly with a repeated figure. Flute and clarinet play a melody that the piano later takes over. Rachmaninoff and Stokowski recorded these opening phrases in Philadelphia in 1929; Richter and Wisłocki recorded them with the Warsaw Philharmonic in 1959.

How the recordings are matched

The matching follows sequences of pitches, using a method called dynamic time warping. Every recording is mapped to the reference, so switching between two versions goes through that shared clock. Where several reference times match one point, the middle time is used.

  • Rachmaninoff, Stokowski, Philadelphia Orchestra, 1929: match score 0.854. Matched passages available.

Switching stays within the matched span, and trimmed recordings keep their original clock times. Match scores describe similarity in the sound.
