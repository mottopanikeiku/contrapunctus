The comparison

Cortot recorded the Black Keys and Butterfly études together on one Victor side on 22 April 1920; Edward Neeman’s separate Black Keys recording was released by Musopen, with only an approximate source date of 2014. This comparison matches Neeman’s 0–108.5 seconds to Cortot’s 3.5–95.5 seconds, excluding Cortot’s lead-in and the Butterfly that begins around 96.5 seconds; the uncut historical stream still contains both pieces. Median alignment confidence is 0.833, usable with caution: surface noise and repeated pitch patterns make local matches, especially around Neeman’s 80–86 seconds and the closing decay, less secure. The passage points invite broad phrase comparisons, not exact bar matches or a precise local-tempo ranking.
