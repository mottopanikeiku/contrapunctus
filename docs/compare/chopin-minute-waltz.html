The comparison

Rachmaninoff recorded the waltz for Victor on 5 April 1923; Muriel Nguyen Xuan’s recording is dated 2007 and Olga Gurevich’s approximately 2014. The files include different amounts of final decay and silence, so the alignment trims their tails rather than treating file length as playing time. Listen for where the running surface relaxes, then resumes.

Alignment evidence & limitations

Chroma features describe pitch-class content. Dynamic time warping matches their sequence, then interpolates between sampled pairs of recording times. When switching from a non-reference recording, its map is inverted first, then the chosen performer’s map is applied. Repeated timestamps use the midpoint of the matching reference span.

  • Gurevich, c. 2014: median chroma confidence 0.946. Automatic alignment remains provisional.
  • Nguyen Xuan, 2007: median chroma confidence 0.932. Automatic alignment remains provisional.

Trims retain their original recording offsets. No timing is extrapolated outside a map. Confidence measures feature similarity, not historical or musical certainty.
