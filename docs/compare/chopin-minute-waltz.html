The comparison

Chopin opens the Minute Waltz with the right hand playing a short figure of notes that circles back on itself. A slower middle section, marked sostenuto, comes between the fast passages. Rachmaninoff, Olga Gurevich and Muriel Nguyen Xuan each play this middle section before the trill leads back into the opening figure.

    The right hand turns around A♭, then moves into a running melody. Bass notes and two answering chords keep up the waltz accompaniment.

  2. 02

    Sostenuto middle section

    0:40 in the reference

    The quick notes stretch into a sustained melody, marked sostenuto. The left hand keeps its three-beat dance underneath.

  3. 03

    Return of the opening figure

    1:18 in the reference

    The circling figure returns after the long trill, and the bass rejoins it. The running melody starts again.

How the recordings are matched

The matching follows sequences of pitches, using a method called dynamic time warping. Every recording is mapped to the reference, so switching between two versions goes through that shared clock. Where several reference times match one point, the middle time is used.

  • Olga Gurevich, c. 2014: match score 0.946. Matched passages available.
  • Muriel Nguyen Xuan, 2007: match score 0.932. Matched passages available.

Switching stays within the matched span, and trimmed recordings keep their original clock times. Match scores describe similarity in the sound.
