The comparison

Stokowski recorded the Philadelphia Orchestra on 12 November 1934; Monteux’s San Francisco recording dates from 3–4 March 1942. The comparison uses Stokowski’s first disc side against the opening of Monteux’s complete movement. Alignment is restricted to the inspected opening phrases, Stokowski 2.8–65 seconds and Monteux 2.2–42 seconds; later music and whole-movement speed comparisons are excluded.

Alignment evidence & limitations

Chroma features describe pitch-class content. Dynamic time warping matches their sequence, then interpolates between sampled pairs of recording times. When switching from a non-reference recording, its map is inverted first, then the chosen performer’s map is applied. Repeated timestamps use the midpoint of the matching reference span.

  • Monteux, 1942: median chroma confidence 0.874. Automatic alignment remains provisional.

Trims retain their original recording offsets. No timing is extrapolated outside a map. Confidence measures feature similarity, not historical or musical certainty.
