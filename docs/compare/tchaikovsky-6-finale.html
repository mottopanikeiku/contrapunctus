The comparison

Furtwängler and the Berlin Philharmonic recorded the symphony in Berlin in October–November 1938; the comparison reuses the site’s Musopen Symphony finale, whose session date is not established. The provisional map aligns Musopen’s 0–615 seconds with Furtwängler’s 0–600, excluding terminal tails, and has median chroma confidence 0.875. Local confidence falls below 0.8 in parts of the quiet music, especially around reference 330–355 seconds and the ending, so compare broader phrases rather than trusting each switching instant. Commons explicitly applies territorial PD-EU-audio to Furtwängler’s recording, but US status and the terms of the now-unavailable upstream transfer remain unestablished.

Chroma features describe pitch-class content. Dynamic time warping matches their sequence, then interpolates between sampled pairs of recording times.
