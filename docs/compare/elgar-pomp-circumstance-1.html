The comparison

Elgar’s Queen’s Hall recording and Stock’s Chicago recording were both made in 1926 and issued by Victor in 1927, as the session sources and contemporary trade bulletins document. Stock’s label explicitly credits grand organ; the Marine Band plays an unidentified concert-band arrangement. Stock’s map covers 0:05–4:18 of both transfers, while band switching covers only the returning march and final trio, Elgar 2:08–4:18 against band 3:20–5:43, avoiding the unmatched earlier repetition. Band pitch-class features are shifted two semitones for matching, but the audio is unaltered.

Alignment evidence & limitations

Chroma features describe pitch-class content. Dynamic time warping matches their sequence, then interpolates between sampled pairs of recording times. When switching from a non-reference recording, its map is inverted first, then the chosen performer’s map is applied. Repeated timestamps use the midpoint of the matching reference span.
