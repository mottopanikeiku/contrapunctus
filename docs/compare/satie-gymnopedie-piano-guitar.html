The comparison

Robin Alciatore plays Satie’s piano original in a source dated approximately 2008; Michael Laucke’s guitar source is dated 23 July 2001, neither date establishing the recording session. Laucke adapts the bass, chords and held melody to six strings in A rather than D, so the alignment compensates for five semitones without altering playback. The map covers both complete files, 183.61 and 171.89 seconds, with provisional median confidence 0.954; the sparse opening and final decay are less secure than the middle phrases. Compare phrase spacing and the survival of held tones, not raw volume or identical voicings.

Chroma features describe pitch-class content. Dynamic time warping matches their sequence, then interpolates between sampled pairs of recording times.
