The comparison

Rachmaninoff’s 1919 Edison performance is the timeline reference for his 1921 acoustic Victor and 1928 electrical remake, with Martin Wörner’s May 2005 recording as a modern counterpart. DAHR identifies the 1928 issued take 23, but Victor 814 can contain either of two 1921 takes and this transfer does not resolve which. The complete access files run about3:48, 3:30, 3:31 and 3:53 respectively; those lengths include leads and decays, while the maps compare musical position. Chroma feature transposition, where reported, changes alignment features only: no audio has been retuned.

Alignment evidence & limitations

Chroma features describe pitch-class content. Dynamic time warping matches their sequence, then interpolates between sampled pairs of recording times. When switching from a non-reference recording, its map is inverted first, then the chosen performer’s map is applied. Repeated timestamps use the midpoint of the matching reference span.

  • Rachmaninoff, 1921 (Victor): median chroma confidence 0.967. Automatic alignment remains provisional.
  • Rachmaninoff, 1928 (electrical): median chroma confidence 0.923. Automatic alignment remains provisional.
  • Martin Wörner, 2005: median chroma confidence 0.904. Automatic alignment remains provisional.

Trims retain their original recording offsets. No timing is extrapolated outside a map. Confidence measures feature similarity, not historical or musical certainty.
