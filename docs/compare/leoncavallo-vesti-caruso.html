The comparison

Caruso recorded Gramophone matrix 2875b with pianist Salvatore Cottone in Milan on 30 November 1902; the orchestral transfer is attributed through Victor 88061 to C-4317, recorded in New York on 17 March 1907. Its exact master has not been confirmed from a legible runout, so the source’s acoustic attribution is retained with that qualification rather than independently excluding every later dub. The provisional map compares only 5–114 seconds of the piano side with 7–139 seconds of the orchestral side; complete streams retain their different openings and later continuations. A two-semitone adjustment applies to alignment features only, without changing playback or establishing original tuning, transfer speed or a whole-side tempo difference.

Chroma features describe pitch-class content. Dynamic time warping matches their sequence, then interpolates between sampled pairs of recording times.
