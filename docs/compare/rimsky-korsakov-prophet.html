The comparison

Chaliapin recorded this Pushkin setting with Percy Pitt in 1921 and Albert Coates in 1927. The 1927 side was first published in 1928 and is used on territorial European public-domain grounds; US recording status is unestablished. The map compares 0:04–3:44 in 1921 with 0:04–4:19 in 1927, omitting lead-in and residual noise. It is less certain around 2:54–2:55 of the earlier side, and recording technology cannot be isolated from conductors, interpretation and transfer choices.

Chroma features describe pitch-class content. Dynamic time warping matches their sequence, then interpolates between sampled pairs of recording times.
