The comparison

Grieg recorded an abridged version of his piano piece in Paris on 2 May 1903; Harald Vetter’s complete performance dates from 2006. The historical disc is US public domain, and Vetter’s recording carries CC BY 4.0 at both IMSLP and Commons. Only the shared opening passage is mapped: Grieg 0:18–1:28 against Vetter 0:21.6–1:56.31, excluding the unreliable opening, cut ending and modern performance’s contrasting middle. Historical pitch instability and undocumented transfer choices limit exact tempo comparisons; the two whole-file durations are not comparable.

Alignment evidence & limitations

Chroma features describe pitch-class content. Dynamic time warping matches their sequence, then interpolates between sampled pairs of recording times. When switching from a non-reference recording, its map is inverted first, then the chosen performer’s map is applied. Repeated timestamps use the midpoint of the matching reference span.

  • Harald Vetter · complete performance: median chroma confidence 0.897. Automatic alignment remains provisional.

Trims retain their original recording offsets. No timing is extrapolated outside a map. Confidence measures feature similarity, not historical or musical certainty.
