The comparison

Judith Bokor’s Columbia F 87 side is dated only circa 1925 in its upstream discography; the exact day and catalogue number on Commons conflict with that source. Alisa Weilerstein and Jason Yoder played Le Cygne at the White House on 4 November 2009. The provisional map covers Bokor 0–150 seconds against Weilerstein 4–155 seconds, excluding opening room sound and post-performance sound; a one-semitone feature correction aligns the pitch profiles without changing either audio stream. Bokor’s recording is admitted under European recording-rights expiry, not composition-only tags or an established US first-publication date.

Alignment evidence & limitations

Chroma features describe pitch-class content. Dynamic time warping matches their sequence, then interpolates between sampled pairs of recording times. When switching from a non-reference recording, its map is inverted first, then the chosen performer’s map is applied. Repeated timestamps use the midpoint of the matching reference span.

  • Alisa Weilerstein and Jason Yoder: median chroma confidence 0.882. Automatic alignment remains provisional.

Trims retain their original recording offsets. No timing is extrapolated outside a map. Confidence measures feature similarity, not historical or musical certainty.
