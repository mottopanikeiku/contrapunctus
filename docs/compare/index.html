A listening instrument

One transport.
More than one reading.

  1. Choose a performance. Its recording plays through the Hall’s shared player.
  2. Change the hands. The alignment seeks the other recording to the corresponding phrase.
  3. Keep the evidence in view. Automatic chroma alignment is provisional and may be wrong across cuts. A duration is a measurement, not an explanation.
