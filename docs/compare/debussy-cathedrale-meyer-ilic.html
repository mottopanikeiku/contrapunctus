The comparison

Marcelle Meyer recorded this prelude at Salle Adyar, Paris, on 15 February 1956; Ivan Ilić recorded it in Paris in April 2006. Both play the solo-piano work, whose broad chordal melody returns later as a quiet, lower echo. The automatic alignment covers 0–330 seconds of Meyer against 0–310 seconds of Ilić, excluding the final decay; it remains provisional, not a score alignment. Meyer’s recording is used under the documented European recording-rights expiry claim, not an assertion of US public-domain status.

Alignment evidence & limitations

Chroma features describe pitch-class content. Dynamic time warping matches their sequence, then interpolates between sampled pairs of recording times. When switching from a non-reference recording, its map is inverted first, then the chosen performer’s map is applied. Repeated timestamps use the midpoint of the matching reference span.
