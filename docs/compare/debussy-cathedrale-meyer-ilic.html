The comparison

The piece opens with bare fifths in the manner of bells. The piano then imitates an organ, with C major chords spread across the keyboard, before the music returns to a quiet echo. Marcelle Meyer and Ivan Ilić recorded La cathédrale engloutie in Paris, fifty years apart.

  3. 03

    Melody in C major chords

    The organ-like tune is set in full C major chords. Their top notes carry the melody above a deep bass.

  4. 04

    Return of the melody, pianissimo

    4:38 in the reference

    The chordal tune returns lower and pianissimo, over a moving bass. Debussy marks it as an echo of the earlier phrase.

How the recordings are matched

The matching follows sequences of pitches, using a method called dynamic time warping. Every recording is mapped to the reference, so switching between two versions goes through that shared clock. Where several reference times match one point, the middle time is used.
