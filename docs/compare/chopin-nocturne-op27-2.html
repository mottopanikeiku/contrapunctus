The comparison

Pachmann recorded the nocturne on his own Baldwin piano at Hayes on26 June 1925; its HMV DB 860 issue was already reviewed in The Gramophone that December. The review praises the performance’s spirit while observing uneven recorded sonority and explicitly says he does not speak on the disc. Frank Lévy’s Musopen recording supplies the modern timeline reference. The files run about4:36 and 6:18, but musical alignment—not raw duration—is needed to compare the same returning phrase.

Alignment evidence & limitations

Chroma features describe pitch-class content. Dynamic time warping matches their sequence, then interpolates between sampled pairs of recording times. When switching from a non-reference recording, its map is inverted first, then the chosen performer’s map is applied. Repeated timestamps use the midpoint of the matching reference span.
