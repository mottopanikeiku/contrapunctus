The comparison

Ravel directs the Lamoureux orchestra in the transfer attributed to 1930; BnF confirms the performer combination and a published Polydor issue by 1935. Fricsay’s reference file has conflicting1950s dates, so no exact session year is asserted for it. The access streams last about15:52 and 13:26 respectively, including their recorded leads and tails; the alignment follows shared musical position rather than multiplying every moment by one ratio. Tzipine and the unverified Munch/Lamoureux alternatives are not included.

Alignment evidence & limitations

Chroma features describe pitch-class content. Dynamic time warping matches their sequence, then interpolates between sampled pairs of recording times. When switching from a non-reference recording, its map is inverted first, then the chosen performer’s map is applied. Repeated timestamps use the midpoint of the matching reference span.

  • Ravel, 1930 (Lamoureux): median chroma confidence 0.923. Automatic alignment remains provisional.

Trims retain their original recording offsets. No timing is extrapolated outside a map. Confidence measures feature similarity, not historical or musical certainty.
