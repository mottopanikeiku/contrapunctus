The comparison

Stokowski’s Philadelphia recording dates from 1934 and Robinson’s London session from 18 January 1945. The United States Air Force Band plays Lawrence Odom’s arrangement on its 1996 album Christmas on Stage and Screen. Only the shared principal-waltz passage is aligned: the unequal introductions and different record-side endings are excluded, and no historical sides are concatenated.

Alignment evidence & limitations

Chroma features describe pitch-class content. Dynamic time warping matches their sequence, then interpolates between sampled pairs of recording times. When switching from a non-reference recording, its map is inverted first, then the chosen performer’s map is applied. Repeated timestamps use the midpoint of the matching reference span.

  • Robinson, 1945 · side 1: median chroma confidence 0.968. Automatic alignment remains provisional.
  • US Air Force Band, 1996: median chroma confidence 0.952. Automatic alignment remains provisional.

Trims retain their original recording offsets. No timing is extrapolated outside a map. Confidence measures feature similarity, not historical or musical certainty.
