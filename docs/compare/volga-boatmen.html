The comparison

Chaliapin recorded this bass-and-orchestra arrangement with George W. Byng in 1922 and Albert Coates in 1927. The comparison follows 0:05–3:00 of the acoustic side and 0:05–3:10 of the electrical remake, not their residual noise tails. Repeated harmony makes the automatic path locally ambiguous around 2:00–2:25 in 1922, so its apparent stalls and surges are not evidence of sudden tempo changes. The 1927 recording was first published in 1928 and is used as territorial European public domain, with US status unestablished.
