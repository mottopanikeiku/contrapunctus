The comparison

Rachmaninoff recorded the concerto with Stokowski and the Philadelphia Orchestra on 10 April 1929; the reference is Richter with Wisłocki and the Warsaw National Philharmonic Orchestra in 1959. This comparison uses only HMV D.B. 1333’s first side, matching Richter’s opening 0–207 seconds to 3–179 seconds of the historical transfer, not reconstructing the complete movement. The provisional map has median chroma confidence 0.934 after a one-semitone adjustment to the historical pitch-class features alone. Playback remains untouched, so the timing describes these supplied transfers rather than speed-corrected historical performances; the Archive also reports wow and minor distortion.

Alignment evidence & limitations

Chroma features describe pitch-class content. Dynamic time warping matches their sequence, then interpolates between sampled pairs of recording times. When switching from a non-reference recording, its map is inverted first, then the chosen performer’s map is applied. Repeated timestamps use the midpoint of the matching reference span.

  • Rachmaninoff / Stokowski · first side: median chroma confidence 0.934. Automatic alignment remains provisional.

Trims retain their original recording offsets. No timing is extrapolated outside a map. Confidence measures feature similarity, not historical or musical certainty.
