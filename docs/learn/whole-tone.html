What happens when every scale step is equal?

Whole-tone harmony: no privileged semitone

Hear itReadContinue

Listen to the equal-sized steps: none is the small step that usually leans towards a neighbour.

Synthesised example

The idea in detail

A whole-tone scale divides the octave into six equal whole tones: C–D–E–F♯–G♯–A♯–C. It contains no semitone and no perfect fifth. Every note has the same interval environment, placing C, D and F♯ on equal terms within the collection. A bass pedal, accent or melodic recurrence can still establish a centre.

Only two collectionsTranspose a whole-tone collection up a semitone and obtain the other one. Transpose it up a whole tone and recover the original pitch classes in a different order. Messiaen classified it as his first mode of limited transposition.

Taking alternate notes produces an augmented triad: C–E–G♯. Its three major thirds divide the octave equally, making its inversions sound less differentiated than those of a major triad. D–F♯–A♯ supplies the other augmented triad within the same whole-tone collection. Chords can move in parallel while staying inside the scale. The bass and the duration of each sonority can supply direction independently of dominant–tonic functions.

Debussy’s piano prelude Voiles, published in Book I in 1910, uses whole-tone material for most of its span, with a contrasting pentatonic passage. The contrast matters because a five-note collection restores unequal interval sizes, including minor thirds, which the whole-tone field excludes. Its title can mean either “sails” or “veils”. A sustained B♭ gives the symmetrical field a reference point outside conventional B♭ major or minor.

A Russian precedentGlinka used whole-tone material in Ruslan and Lyudmila (1842), notably in the supernatural associations around Chernomor. Russian composers developed that vocabulary before Debussy’s piano preludes.

Whole-tone passages can also arise locally inside tonal music: an altered dominant may share several of their notes. The analyst must distinguish a complete governing collection from a brief chord that happens to fit one. An ornamental chromatic note can occur within a whole-tone passage while its stable degrees remain intact. In the example, the two augmented triads partition all six notes. Their relationship is one of register and pitch level, independent of a leading note or a perfect-fifth root relation.
