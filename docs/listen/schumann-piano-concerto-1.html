Piano Concerto in A minor, Op. 54I. Allegro affettuoso

1841; revised 1845 · A minor · Piano and orchestra
Sviatoslav Richter (piano), Warsaw National Philharmonic Orchestra, Witold Rowicki (conductor) · recorded 1959 on Commons; 11–12 October 1958 in the Richter discography. (disputed) · About this recording

When is the soloist also an accompanist?

In the music 0:00

No long orchestral introduction

The score begins with one orchestral attack and a descending piano flourish, then turns towards a quieter melody. These contrasting gestures establish the soloist’s involvement without a long orchestral introduction.

How we know

General description of this stretch. Michael Steinberg’s BSO note describes this opening sequence directly from the score.

Around the music
A possible name in notes

The theme’s C–B–A–A has been read as “CHiArA”, an Italian form of Clara’s name: German notation calls B natural H. This is an interpretation, not a meaning established by a surviving explanation from Schumann.

Listening notes

Schumann brings the piano into the opening almost immediately, then shares the principal melody between soloist and orchestra. Can you follow the tune when the busiest part is not the one carrying it? Select a note to hear its moment.
