A low, quiet opening

The opening builds from a quiet foundation in the low register. This is Lawrence Odom’s concert-band transcription: listen for how sustained sounds support the shorter figures, without expecting the string sections of Debussy’s original orchestration.

How we know

Located by measuring this recording. The analysis finds a low relative level and predominantly low-frequency sound in the opening; Commons credits Odom’s band transcription.

Around the music
The sea from childhood

Debussy visited Cannes during childhood summers and entered the Paris Conservatoire at ten. He began La Mer in 1903 and completed it in 1905; his attraction to the sea long preceded the score.

Listening notes

The first of Debussy’s three symphonic sketches is heard here in Lawrence Odom’s concert-band transcription, not the original orchestration. How do short figures and overlapping rhythms accumulate into its broad closing passage? Select a note to hear its moment.
