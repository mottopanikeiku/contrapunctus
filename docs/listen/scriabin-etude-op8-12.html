How can a melody sing above a busy accompaniment?

A line above the activity

Try following the melody as one continuing line, rather than giving every accompanying note equal attention. The study asks the pianist to keep that line distinct while covering wide distances on the keyboard.

How we know

General description of this stretch. The score outline describes a chordal melody above a widely spaced accompaniment. This cue concerns the texture, not an individual note or hand movement at a precise second: https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89tude_op._8_no_12_de_Scriabine

Around the music
A publisher’s support

Mitrofan Belyayev heard Scriabin’s music in 1894 and began publishing his works and paying advances. Their surviving correspondence records not just encouragement but negotiations over deadlines and revisions.

Listening notes

A short étude that asks for weight as well as speed: the right-hand melody is projected in octaves over widely spaced left-hand figures. Listen for the quieter middle and the heavier return of the opening. Select a note to hear its moment.
