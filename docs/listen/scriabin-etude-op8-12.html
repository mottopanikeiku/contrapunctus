Octaves over a wide bass

The right hand projects the melody in octaves while the left covers wide distances beneath it. Listen for the singing top line rather than treating every note in the accompaniment as equally important.

How we know

General description of this stretch. D♯ minor estimated enharmonically as E♭ minor, r=0.94 at 0:00; 4.0–4.8 onsets/s at 0:00–0:10 support the continuous accompanying motion.

Around the music
A Moscow-trained pianist

Scriabin graduated from the Moscow Conservatory as a pianist in 1892. Rachmaninoff was a fellow student; Scriabin’s first published works likewise centred on the piano.

Listening notes

A short étude that asks for weight as well as speed: the right-hand melody is projected in octaves over widely spaced left-hand figures. Listen for the quieter middle and the heavier return of the opening. Select a note to hear its moment.
