Short figures, longer tunes

The opening sets a short bell-like figure beside a longer melody. Keep both kinds of material in mind: Mahler can make a brief rhythmic interruption as important to the movement as a complete tune.

How we know

General description of this stretch. Bettie Jo Basinger’s Utah Symphony guide describes the opening figure and first theme. This is an opening-passage guide, not a timed identification of an instrumental entrance. https://usuo.org/explore/2015/05/mahler-4-listening-guide/

Around the music
Composed between conducting seasons

Mahler began the Fourth during his 1899 summer holiday at Bad Aussee and completed it the following summer. His conducting schedule left a limited interval between operatic seasons for composition.

Listening notes

Mahler begins with sleigh bells and a G major violin tune, then builds a sonata movement from material that first sounds almost domestic. Listen for how the same themes change character when played against one another. Select a note to hear its moment.
