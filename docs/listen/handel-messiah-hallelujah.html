How does a chorus gain force without simply singing louder?

Together and in pursuit

Throughout the chorus, distinguish voices moving together from lines that enter one after another. Handel varies the force of a declaration by changing its texture, not only the weight of the sound.

How we know

General description of this stretch. The score alternates chordal declarations with imitative writing. This is a whole-chorus listening guide; no sung word or individual entrance is assigned to this timestamp.

Around the music
Jennens supplies the words

Charles Jennens assembled the scriptural text that Handel set in 1741. The three parts do not enact one continuous plot: they move from prophecy and birth through suffering and resurrection to the believer’s response.

Listening notes

The chorus that closes Part II alternates three kinds of speech: a tune sung together, a line passed between voices, and long notes held above moving counterpoint. Its force comes from those changes of texture, not from singing everything loudly. Select a note to hear its moment.
