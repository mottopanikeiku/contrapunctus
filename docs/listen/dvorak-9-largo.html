Chords before the song

The opening contrasts a sequence of chords with the long melody that follows. Keep both shapes in mind: the introduction is not merely a count-in, and its chordal writing will matter again near the close.

How we know

General description of this stretch. The Antonín Dvořák work guide describes the introductory chords and their later return. This identifies the opening design, not a precisely timed instrumental entrance. https://www.antonin-dvorak.cz/en/work/symphony-no-9/

Around the music
Written in New York

Dvořák composed the symphony in New York between 10 January and 24 May 1893. It belongs to the first year of his American appointment; the score was complete before his summer in Spillville, Iowa.

Listening notes

The cor anglais melody became “Goin’ Home” only after Dvořák’s death. Around that apparently simple song he builds a slow movement with a minor-key middle, a sudden orchestral confrontation and a retreat into chamber music. Select a note to hear its moment.
