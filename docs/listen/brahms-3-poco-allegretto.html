A song in three

Brahms gives this movement a three-beat measure rather than a fast scherzo’s pace. Follow the long melodic phrases against the smaller movements of the accompaniment; their points of arrival need not coincide.

How we know

General description of this stretch. The movement’s score is in 3/8 and marked Poco allegretto. This describes its recurring phrasing, not a timed instrumental entrance.

Around the music
A summer in Wiesbaden

Brahms wrote the Third Symphony in Wiesbaden in the summer of 1883. His First Symphony had reached performance in 1876, the Second in 1877; the Third followed six years later.

Listening notes

Instead of a scherzo, Brahms writes a C minor song in three beats. Trumpets and timpani remain silent while the melody passes between the strings and solo winds. Select a note to hear its moment.
