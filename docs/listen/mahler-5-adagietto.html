Fourth of five movements

The Adagietto stands between the Fifth Symphony’s scherzo and finale. Its forces are strings and harp: the winds, brass and percussion of the surrounding movements are absent.

How we know

Documented fact. The score specifies the movement order and forces; the Mahler Foundation’s movement account describes the reduced scoring. This note concerns the whole movement.

Around the music
A letter without words?

The conductor Willem Mengelberg wrote in his score that the Adagietto was Mahler’s declaration of love to Alma Schindler, sent to her in manuscript instead of a letter. The story rests on Mengelberg’s word alone.

Listening notes

Between the Fifth Symphony’s scherzo and finale, Mahler writes for strings and harp alone. Follow the long phrases through their swells, withdrawals and return. Select a note to hear its moment.
