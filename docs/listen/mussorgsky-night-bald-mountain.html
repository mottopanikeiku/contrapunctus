In the music 0:00

Motion before a broad tune

The opening combines rapid figures with heavier, separated gestures. Listen to the relationship between the moving surface and the lower accents: different rates of activity can coexist without all the parts doubling one another.

How we know

General description of this stretch. This describes the opening texture of Rimsky-Korsakov’s version; no individual instrument entrance is inferred from the recording.

Around the music
An officer leaves service

Mussorgsky received a Guards commission in 1856 and resigned it in 1858. He studied composition with Balakirev outside the conservatory system and began civil-service work in 1863.

Listening notes

This is Rimsky-Korsakov’s 1886 concert reworking, not Mussorgsky’s original 1867 orchestral score. How does the long quiet ending alter your memory of the witches’ gathering? Select a note to hear its moment.
