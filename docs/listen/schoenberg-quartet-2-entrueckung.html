An unsettled opening

Across the instrumental introduction, follow the chromatic lines without expecting an immediate home-key cadence. The movement will not avoid tonal reference altogether, but its opening suspends the usual harmonic bearings.

How we know

Placed from the score’s proportions; timing approximate. Therese Muxeneder’s Arnold Schönberg Center account identifies the introduction’s suspended tonality. The report shows a quiet opening, but its unstable key estimates are not evidence of particular harmonies.

Around the music
George’s seventh ring

Both poems in the quartet come from Stefan George’s Der siebente Ring, published in 1907. The last two movements set “Litanei” and “Entrückung”, adding a soprano to the four string players.

Listening notes

“I feel air from another planet”: Stefan George’s words accompany Schoenberg’s departure from a governing key. Four strings and a soprano carry the finale; the twelve-tone method is still more than a decade away. Select a note to hear its moment.
