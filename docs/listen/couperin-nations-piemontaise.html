How do three musical lines become a conversation?

In the music 0:00

Three parts, four players

Violin and flute carry the two upper parts; viol and keyboard share the bass. Through the opening, listen for the relationship between those lines: one can sustain a note while another moves, making harmony out of their overlap.

How we know

General description of this stretch. The score has two upper parts and a bass; Commons identifies the four players in this recording.

Around the music
An inherited organ loft

Couperin’s father Charles was organist of Saint-Gervais in Paris. After Charles died in 1679, the post was reserved for the young François; Lalande held it until he could take over.

Listening notes

Couperin places an Italian-style trio sonata before the French dances of <i>La Piémontaise</i>, played here by four musicians sharing three written parts. How do the two upper lines exchange the lead above their shared bass? Select a note to hear its moment.
