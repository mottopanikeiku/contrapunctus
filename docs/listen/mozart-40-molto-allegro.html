Symphony No. 40, K. 550I. Molto allegro

Already in motion

The movement begins without a slow introduction. Listen for the opening melody’s repeated notes and small downward step against the moving accompaniment; a compact figure supplies material for much of the later argument.

How we know

General description of this stretch. The score’s opening figure and its later development are discussed by Matthew Lorenzon for ABC Classic. This describes the opening passage rather than a timed violin entrance. https://www.abc.net.au/listen/classic/read-and-watch/music-reads/mozart-symphony-no-40-sonata-form/10160386

Around the music
Three symphonies in one summer

Mozart composed his last three symphonies in the summer of 1788. The G minor symphony sits between No. 39 in E♭ major and No. 41 in C major; their sequence does not establish a private narrative programme.

Listening notes

No ceremonial introduction: the accompaniment is already moving when the violin tune arrives. The same falling semitone supplies melody, transition and contrapuntal argument across this compact sonata movement. Select a note to hear its moment.
