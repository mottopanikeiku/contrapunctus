Prelude in C♯ minor, Op. 3 No. 2

1892 · C♯ minor · Piano
Sergei Rachmaninoff (piano) · recorded 1919-04-24 · About this recording

What makes the same opening sound larger on its return?

In the music 0:00

Three notes and an answer

The opening’s low A–G♯–C♯ figure is set against quieter answering chords. Listen for the contrast between strongly projected notes and the softer lines around them; the score gives both roles to a single pianist.

How we know

General description of this stretch. The Mutopia score specifies the three-note figure and answering chords; the old recording’s pitch estimates are not used to identify individual notes.

Around the music
Nineteen and newly qualified

Rachmaninoff wrote the Prelude in 1892, the year he graduated from the Moscow Conservatory. It was published as the second of five Morceaux de fantaisie, Op. 3, dedicated to his teacher Anton Arensky.

Listening notes

Rachmaninoff plays his 1892 Prelude in a 1919 Edison recording, moving from a slow opening through an agitated middle to an enlarged return. What changes when the opening material spreads across a much wider chordal texture? Select a note to hear its moment.
