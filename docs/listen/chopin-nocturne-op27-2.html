Singing above broken chords

Across the opening, broken chords support a slower singing line. Chopin marks the piece “Lento sostenuto” — slow and sustained. Listen for how the melody connects notes whose piano sound begins decaying as soon as they are struck.

How we know

Placed from the score’s proportions; timing approximate. The score specifies this opening texture and marking. The report places a relatively quiet D♭-centred span at the start; individual notes are not aligned.

Around the music
A Parisian dedication

The two Op. 27 Nocturnes were published in 1836 and dedicated to Countess Thérèse d’Apponyi, whose Paris salon Chopin frequented. The dedication identifies a patron, not a hidden subject for the music.

Listening notes

A rocking left hand supports a melody that repeatedly grows a second voice. Listen for the difference between the notes that carry the phrase and the ornaments that delay their arrival. Select a note to hear its moment.
