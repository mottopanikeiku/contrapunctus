A refrain to remember

The strings establish E major with short phrases and their quieter echoes. This is the ritornello: orchestral material that will return between the solo episodes, sometimes shortened or moved into another key.

How we know

General description of this stretch. E-major estimate r=0.86–0.93 throughout 0:00–0:30, with E/G♯/B prominent. Loudness alternates −14.6 at 0:05, −20.1 at 0:10, −13.0 at 0:15 and −21.1 at 0:25, matching the score’s echo contrasts.

Around the music
The Pietà’s violin teacher

Vivaldi became violin teacher at Venice’s Ospedale della Pietà in 1703. The institution cared for abandoned children and maintained an exceptionally trained ensemble of girls and women; teaching and composing belonged to the same employment.

Listening notes

The opening refrain gives the orchestra something to return to while the solo violin pursues the birds, streams and storm of the accompanying sonnet. Programme and concerto form occupy the same bars. Select a note to hear its moment.
