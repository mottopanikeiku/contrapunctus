A refrain to remember

Remember the shape of the opening phrases. This is the ritornello, or returning refrain: material that links the episodes, sometimes coming back in a shortened form rather than as a complete repeat.

How we know

General description of this stretch. The University of North Georgia’s Resonances chapter explains the opening refrain and its shortened returns. This cue concerns the movement’s design, not a timed instrumental entrance: https://human.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Music/Ethnomusicology/Resonances_-_Engaging_Music_in_its_Cultural_Context_(Morgan-Ellis_Ed.)/02%3A_Music_for_Storytelling/06%3A_Stories_without_Words/6.04%3A_Antonio_Vivaldi_-_The_Four_Seasons_Spring

Around the music
The Pietà’s violin teacher

Vivaldi became violin teacher at Venice’s Ospedale della Pietà in 1703, the year he was ordained a priest. Teaching and instrumental composition were central parts of his work there.

Listening notes

The opening refrain returns between changing episodes. Birds, streams and a storm belong to the accompanying sonnet, not to a story invented by later listeners; Vivaldi links its images to the musical score. Select a note to hear its moment.
