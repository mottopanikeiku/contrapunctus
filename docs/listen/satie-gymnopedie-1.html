Bass, chord, empty beat

The left hand places a bass on the first beat and a chord on the second, leaving the third beat without a new accompaniment attack. G major seventh alternates with D major seventh. The melody crosses this regular pattern rather than supplying a note for every beat.

How we know

Around the music
Montmartre, not the Conservatoire

Satie wrote the three Gymnopédies in 1888, during his early years in Montmartre’s cabaret world. He had left the Paris Conservatoire without completing its training. Piano work at venues including the Chat Noir paid for a life outside the official institutions.

Listening notes

A bass note on beat one, a chord on beat two, and a melody that crosses the repeated pattern. Satie’s first Gymnopédie lets unresolved sevenths remain in the harmony instead of demanding their immediate resolution. Select a note to hear its moment.
