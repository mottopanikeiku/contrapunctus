Around the music
Outside the Conservatoire

Satie left home for Montmartre in 1887 and wrote the Gymnopédies in 1888. Two periods at the Paris Conservatoire had ended without a qualification; cabaret work became part of his livelihood.

Listening notes

Gmaj7 and Dmaj7 alternate beneath a melody that holds notes across the accompaniment; the repeated span leads to a different final cadence. The score is aligned automatically, with provisional timings not checked by ear. Select a note to hear its moment.
