Alto: all twelve notes

The alto opens alone in B minor, beginning F♯–D–B after a quaver rest. Leaps connect pairs of notes a semitone apart. The complete subject uses all twelve chromatic pitch classes, but its move towards F♯ still gives the line a tonal direction.

How we know

From the score, timed by aligning the score to this recording. Bars 1–4, occurrence 1: Mutopia BWV 869 (1563), notes.lyi, alto. Its subject contains C, C♯, D, D♯, E, F (written E♯), F♯, G, G♯, A, A♯ and B; B♯ also appears as an enharmonic C. The closing G♯ resolves to F♯ in bar 4. Timing aligned automatically.

Around the music
Learning in his brother’s household

After his father died in 1695, Bach moved to Ohrdruf to live with his elder brother Johann Christoph, an organist. He learned the organ under his brother’s guidance while attending the local grammar school and singing in its choir.
