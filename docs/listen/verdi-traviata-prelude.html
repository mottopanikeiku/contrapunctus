The exposed opening

The score opens with quiet, divided high strings rather than the weight of the full orchestra. Compare that exposed texture with the broader melody later in the prelude; it can suggest a vulnerability quite unlike the party scene that follows.

How we know

General description of this stretch. The instrumentation comes from the score; the dramatic reading is an interpretation, not something established by the noisy transfer’s pitch estimates.

Around the music
Venice, March 1853

La traviata was first performed at La Fenice on 6 March 1853, with a libretto by Francesco Maria Piave. Its central figure, Violetta, is a Parisian courtesan whose relationship with Alfredo is constrained by illness and social respectability.

Listening notes

Before Violetta’s party begins, Verdi contrasts exposed high strings with a broader melody that returns later in the opera. How does the prelude change your expectations before any singer appears? Select a note to hear its moment.
