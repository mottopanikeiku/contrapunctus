Pavane pour une infante défunte

A line above the chords

The opening melody stretches across the regular accompanying chords. Listen for the independence of those two rhythms: a long melodic note can continue while the harmony beneath it takes another step.

How we know

General description of this stretch. The piano score gives the melody longer values above a regular accompaniment; no individual note is aligned to the recording.

Around the music
Fauré and a patron’s salon

Ravel wrote the Pavane in 1899 while studying with Fauré. His teacher introduced him to the salon of the Princesse de Polignac, the work’s dedicatee.

Listening notes

Thérèse Dussaut plays Ravel’s 1899 piano original, with its long melody above evenly spaced accompanying chords. How does the tune stretch across that regular pulse? Select a note to hear its moment.
