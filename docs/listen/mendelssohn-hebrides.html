Begin with the lower line

The opening idea is a short falling figure, introduced in the lower orchestral register. Follow its shape and the space before it: those small features offer landmarks as the music expands beyond the opening passage.

How we know

General description of this stretch. The score supplies the falling figure and its low-register setting; this note does not time an instrumental entrance.

Around the music
Before entering the cave

On 7 August 1829 Mendelssohn wrote a family letter containing the overture’s opening twenty-one bars. He visited Fingal’s Cave the following day, so this musical sketch preceded the celebrated visit rather than being composed inside the cave.

Listening notes

A short falling figure generates much of Mendelssohn’s concert overture. How does it remain recognisable as harmony, register and orchestral weight change around it? Select a note to hear its moment.
