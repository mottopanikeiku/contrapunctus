One hand, two levels

The top line moves in longer notes while the broken chords continue beneath it. Listen for the pianist’s balance: the accompaniment must remain active without making the melody sound like merely the highest note of each arpeggio.

How we know

General description of this stretch. Opening F♯/C♯/B♭ pitch classes represent the written G♭/D♭/B♭; F♯-major estimate at 0:05–0:10. Continuous 2.0–3.0 detected onsets/s supports the score’s flowing accompaniment.

Around the music
Music beside the classroom

Schubert trained as a teacher and worked as an assistant in his father’s school. In 1814, while still a teenager, he wrote Gretchen am Spinnrade: voice and piano already had distinct jobs, the singer’s recollection set against the spinning wheel’s motion.

Listening notes

The right hand has two jobs: carrying the melody and keeping the broken chords in motion underneath it. Schubert makes a single pianist sustain a singer, an accompaniment and a bass line at three different levels of weight. Select a note to hear its moment.
