A melody above triplets

The melody begins on B♭, the third of G♭ major, while broken chords move in triplet subdivisions beneath it. Listen for the difference between the long upper notes, the faster inner movement and the slower bass: one pianist sustains all three layers.

Around the music
Music beside the classroom

Schubert worked as an assistant at his father’s school while composing. In 1814 he set Goethe’s Gretchen am Spinnrade; he was seventeen. His early working life combined teaching with songs and instrumental music.

Listening notes

A G♭-major melody rides above triplet-motion broken chords, with a more accented minor middle section and a written-out return.
