A single human voice

This is the first sung movement of the Second Symphony. Mahler asks for “very solemn, but simple”; compare the scale of one vocal line with the orchestral writing around it.

How we know

General description of this stretch. The score establishes the first vocal movement and the marking Sehr feierlich, aber schlicht; no vocal entrance is aligned here.

Around the music
A poem from 1808

“Urlicht” appears in the second volume of Des Knaben Wunderhorn, published in Heidelberg in 1808 by Achim von Arnim and Clemens Brentano. Mahler uses its words for a newly composed song, not a transcription of a folk performance.

Listening notes

After three orchestral movements, an alto gives Mahler’s Second Symphony its first words in a setting of <i>Des Knaben Wunderhorn</i>. How do the solitary voice and hymn-like orchestral writing change the scale of the music? Select a note to hear its moment.
