A bridge between keys

Muted strings begin in the C minor left by the first movement, then move towards E major. Rachmaninoff writes the connection into the music: the slow movement’s key is reached, not simply announced.

How we know

General description of this stretch. No detected attacks in the sustained introduction 0:00–0:25; C minor estimates 0:00–0:15, boundary 0:20, then E/B/G♯ pitch classes at 0:30 and E major from 0:45. Muted-string scoring is documented in the score outline.

Around the music
Heard before the first movement

This Adagio and the finale were first performed in Moscow on 15 December 1900, almost eleven months before the complete concerto. Rachmaninoff played and Siloti conducted at a benefit for the Ladies’ Charity Prison Committee.

Listening notes

A slow movement with a fast centre. The piano’s circling accompaniment passes beneath a melody first shared by flute and clarinet; later the soloist breaks out into a cadenza. Select a note to hear its moment.
