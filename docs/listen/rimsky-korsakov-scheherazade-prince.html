How we know

Around the music
A composer in the navy

Rimsky-Korsakov trained as a naval officer and spent three years at sea. He began his First Symphony before that voyage and later worked as an inspector of naval bands, alongside his composing and teaching.

Listening notes

A broad string melody and a lighter dance share the third movement of Rimsky-Korsakov’s suite. How does the return of the longer line change after the quicker music? Select a note to hear its moment.
