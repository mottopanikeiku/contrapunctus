55 real performances, each with a guide that explains what is happening as it plays. Press play on any of them; when one ends, the next in chronological order follows unless you turn that off.

Free recordings concentrate on music written between about 1700 and 1930, because later music is still in copyright. Composers after 1945 are in Explore.

Music keeps its listener oriented by bringing things back. Listen for what comes back unchanged, and what does not.
