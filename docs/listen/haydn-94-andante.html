A tune to remember

The opening theme uses regular phrases, repeated notes and the notes of a C-major chord. Keep its shape in mind: four variations will change the surrounding activity while preserving points of recognition.

How we know

Placed from the score’s proportions; timing approximate. The score gives the theme and four variations. The report places a quiet C-centred passage at the opening; it does not align individual notes.

Around the music
Haydn in London

Haydn visited London in 1791, writing symphonies for concerts organised by the violinist and impresario Johann Peter Salomon. His last twelve symphonies are known collectively as the London or Salomon symphonies.

Listening notes

The loud chord is only the first interruption. Haydn takes a tune made from a few notes of C major through four variations, changing its scoring, mode and rhythmic density without losing its outline. Select a note to hear its moment.
