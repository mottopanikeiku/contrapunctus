A fast third movement

After the Adagio, the symphony turns to a scherzo in A minor, marked “Sehr schnell” — very fast. Follow the repeated figure beneath its themes: the small-scale motion helps bind the larger sections together.

How we know

General description of this stretch. The score supplies the movement’s key, tempo marking and repeated figures; no instrumental entrance is timed here.

Around the music
An organist still studying

Bruckner became organist at Linz Cathedral in 1855. Already in his thirties, he continued studying harmony and counterpoint with Simon Sechter, then form and orchestration with Otto Kitzler.

Listening notes

The Seventh’s scherzo sets fast, repeated figures around a slower trio. How does Bruckner make changes of pace and scale out of this limited material? Select a note to hear its moment.
