In the music 0:00

Two melodies, not one

In the opening section, the instrumental melody and the tenor prayer have distinct contours. Listen past the sung line to the moving accompaniment: it helps shape the phrase rather than simply doubling the singers.

How we know

General description of this stretch. The score gives the opening orchestral melody and tenor line separate parts; their individual entrances are not aligned here.

Around the music
A school for church musicians

Fauré entered the École Niedermeyer at nine. Saint-Saëns taught him there and introduced him to Schumann, Liszt and Wagner; he began his professional career as an organist in Rennes in 1866.

Listening notes

Fauré’s prayer for rest combines a vocal line with an independent instrumental melody, then recalls the Requiem’s opening movement. How does the accompaniment shape what you hear in the voices? Select a note to hear its moment.
