Piano Sonata No. 30, Op. 109III. Gesangvoll, mit innigster Empfindung

1820 · E major · Piano

A theme with repeats

The opening theme has sixteen bars in two repeated halves. Beethoven marks it “Gesangvoll, mit innigster Empfindung” — songfully, with the most inward feeling. Keep its phrase endings in mind: six variations will change the surface around that framework.

How we know

Placed from the score’s proportions; timing approximate. The score gives the theme and its repeats. Quiet points around 0:35, 1:15, 1:50 and 2:30 help estimate its performed extent; the bars have not been aligned to this recording.

Around the music
For Maximiliane Brentano

The sonata is dedicated to Maximiliane Brentano, daughter of Beethoven’s friends Franz and Antonie Brentano. He had already written a short piano trio for her in 1812.

Listening notes

A sixteen-bar theme supports six variations, then returns without its accumulated decoration. The central problem is how far Beethoven can change rhythm, texture and register while keeping the theme’s harmonic outline audible. Select a note to hear its moment.
