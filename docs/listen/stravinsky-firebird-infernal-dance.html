Kashchei’s enforced dance

In the ballet, the Firebird compels Kashchei and his followers to dance. This recording uses Thomas Knox’s band arrangement; listen to the separated rhythmic gestures without assuming that an orchestral string section is present.

How we know

General description of this stretch. The ballet scenario supplies the action, and Commons credits Knox’s arrangement for the United States Marine Band.

Around the music
From law to orchestration

Stravinsky studied law in Saint Petersburg before pursuing his musical training privately with Rimsky-Korsakov. Their regular work on orchestration in 1905–08 preceded the commission for The Firebird.

Listening notes

Thomas Knox’s concert-band arrangement gives the ballet’s enforced dance to brass, woodwind and percussion rather than the original full orchestra. Can you keep a steady pulse beneath the displaced accents? Select a note to hear its moment.
