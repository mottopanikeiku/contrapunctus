Before the regular dance

The introduction delays the regular waltz accompaniment. In this concert-band arrangement, listen to melody and bass as separate layers rather than expecting the string colours of Tchaikovsky’s original orchestral score.

How we know

General description of this stretch. The score places an introduction and cadenza before the principal waltz. The source credits Lawrence Odom’s band arrangement; no original orchestral entrance is assigned to this recording.

Around the music
Hoffmann through Dumas

The Nutcracker reached Tchaikovsky through Alexandre Dumas’s adaptation of E. T. A. Hoffmann’s story. The ballet’s scenario was prepared by theatre director Ivan Vsevolozhsky and choreographer Marius Petipa.

Listening notes

The ballet’s large ensemble waltz, heard in Lawrence Odom’s concert-band arrangement. Listen for the three-beat accompaniment continuing underneath melodies that stretch across several bars. Select a note to hear its moment.
