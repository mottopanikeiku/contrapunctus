How does plucking change the rhythm of a string quartet?

In the music 0:00

A note’s beginning matters

Pizzicato means plucking a string rather than drawing a bow across it. In this movement, compare the rhythm made by those short attacks with the line of a longer, bowed phrase. A change of technique can alter the character without adding another player.

How we know

General description of this stretch. Stephen Walsh’s quartet note describes the plucked first theme and bowed second theme. This is a movement-wide listening guide, not a timed identification of a bow change: https://www.chambermusicsociety.org/about-the-music/compositions/ravel-string-quartet

Around the music
Dedicated to his teacher

Ravel completed the quartet in April 1903 and dedicated it to Gabriel Fauré. He had joined Fauré’s composition class in 1897 and continued attending as an auditor after losing his formal place at the Conservatoire.

Listening notes

The scherzo sets short, plucked sounds against lines sustained with the bow. A slower middle section reshapes the opening material before the brisk character returns. Select a note to hear its moment.
