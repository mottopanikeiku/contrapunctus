String Quartet in F majorII. Assez vif — Très rythmé

Four plucked instruments

All four players begin pizzicato: plucking the strings rather than bowing them. The short attacks expose the rhythmic disagreement inside the bar, where groups of three quavers compete with groups of two.

How we know

General description of this stretch. opening 0:00–0:05 has 4.6–5.0 onsets/s and centroid 714–728 Hz; the score opens with all four parts pizzicato and competing triple/duple groupings

Around the music
Dedicated to his teacher

Ravel completed the quartet in 1903 and dedicated it to Gabriel Fauré. He had entered Fauré’s composition class in 1897 and continued attending as an auditor after losing his formal place at the Conservatoire.

Listening notes

Four string players begin by putting their bows aside. The scherzo sets plucked attacks against sustained lines, with a slower middle section interrupting the friction between groups of two and three. Select a note to hear its moment.
