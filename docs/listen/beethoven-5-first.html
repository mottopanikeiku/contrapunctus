In the music 0:00

A rest, then four notes

Each opening gesture begins after a rest. Three short notes drive into a held note, but the second held note occupies more written time than the first; the two statements are not exact rhythmic copies.

How we know

General description of this stretch. Separated opening attacks, with onset density falling to 0.4/s at 0:05; E♭/F/G then D/F/C chroma. Score bars 1–5 contain the rests, fermatas and longer second hold.

Around the music
The long premiere concert

The Fifth was first performed at Vienna’s Theater an der Wien on 22 December 1808. The same concert included the Sixth Symphony, the Choral Fantasy and the first public performance of the Fourth Piano Concerto, with Beethoven as soloist.

Listening notes

The four-note opening is not just a tune. Beethoven uses its rhythm as accompaniment, transition and punctuation, so that a whole sonata movement can grow from something the listener remembers after two seconds. Select a note to hear its moment.
