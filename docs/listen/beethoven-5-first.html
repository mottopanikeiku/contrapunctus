How much can Beethoven build from three short notes and one?

A rest, then the motto

After a quaver rest, three Gs fall to a held E♭. Three Fs then fall to D, tied across two bars. The fermatas interrupt the pulse; the second gesture already changes the first rather than copying it mechanically.

Around the music
The long premiere concert

The Fifth was first performed at Vienna’s Theater an der Wien on 22 December 1808. The programme also included the Sixth Symphony, Fourth Piano Concerto and Choral Fantasy. These works shared one concert, not a sequence of separate premieres.

Listening notes

Follow the opening rhythm through a horn call, a lyrical E♭-major theme, fragmented development and an extended C-minor coda.
