A phrase with gaps

Listen for short melodic shapes separated by space rather than one uninterrupted tune. On a piano, a held note is already fading; the wait after an attack helps determine the phrase’s length.

How we know

General description of this stretch. This is an editorial listening guide to the movement’s short gestures. The report shows sparse attacks and low relative level through the opening half-minute.

Around the music
A teacher in Brno

Janáček helped establish Brno’s Organ School in 1881 and directed it until 1919. Teaching and institution-building occupied decades of his career alongside composition.

Listening notes

Janáček wrote this sonata after a worker was killed during demonstrations for a Czech university in Brno. Its second movement builds from a short, returning piano phrase; the interruptions matter as much as the notes. Select a note to hear its moment.
