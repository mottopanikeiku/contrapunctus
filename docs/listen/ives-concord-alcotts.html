In the music 0:00

A hymn at home

The opening presents the sonata’s recurring “human faith” melody in a comparatively direct, chordal setting. Listen for the shape of the tune across the spaces between chords, rather than expecting an unbroken stream of notes.

How we know

General description of this stretch. Kyle Gann’s Ives Society guide identifies the melody in this opening; the analysis shows relatively sparse piano attacks.

Around the music
Three members of one family

Ives’s accompanying essay discusses Bronson Alcott, the philosopher; Louisa May, the novelist; and Elizabeth, the family pianist who was the model for Beth in Little Women. The portrait includes domestic music-making as well as philosophical discussion.

Listening notes

Ives’s portrait of the Alcotts combines hymn-like chords with the rhythm of Beethoven’s Fifth, sometimes setting the piano’s hands in different keys. What lets you recognise a melody when its surroundings disagree? Select a note to hear its moment.
