Touch after the attack

The clavichord’s metal tangents strike the strings and remain in contact while the keys are held. Changes of finger pressure can affect a sounding note. Listen for the relationship between attack, continuation and release, not only the succession of pitches.

How we know

General description of this stretch. The source identifies Joan Benson playing clavichord. The note describes the instrument’s mechanism; it does not claim to detect a particular pressure effect in the recording.

Around the music
A practical book

Bach published the two parts of his Essay on the True Art of Playing Keyboard Instruments in 1753 and 1762. Ornamentation and accompaniment were subjects for written instruction as well as skills transmitted from player to player.

Listening notes

A clavichord can alter a note after the finger has struck it. In this late fantasia, Bach makes an instrument quiet enough for a private room speak in interruptions, pauses and sudden changes of register. Select a note to hear its moment.
