A story in rondo form

Strauss specifies “in rondo form”: recurring themes identify Till while the episodes change around him. Compare the broad opening manner with the shorter, more pointed figures that follow.

How we know

Documented fact. The rondo designation comes from the score title. The report shows a quiet, relatively sustained opening; this cue introduces the overall design.

Around the music
The printed trickster

The earliest surviving written Eulenspiegel stories date from nearly two centuries after the fourteenth-century life attributed to their hero. Strauss draws on the trickster tradition without setting one continuous literary narrative.

Listening notes

A recurring pair of themes gives Strauss’s prankster an identity through disguises, interruptions and a final trial. This complete concert-band performance transfers the orchestral story to winds and percussion. Select a note to hear its moment.
