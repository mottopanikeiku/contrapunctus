An Adagio in three

The variation moves in three beats to the bar, rather than the four of the original Enigma theme. Across the opening, distinguish the steady beat from the longer melodic phrase: the tune need not stop at each bar line.

How we know

General description of this stretch. The metre is specified in the score. This is an opening-span listening suggestion, not a claim about an individual attack.

Around the music
The friend behind Nimrod

August Jaeger encouraged Elgar during the years when he struggled to establish a lasting reputation. This variation portrays their friendship; the complete set is dedicated “to my friends pictured within”.

Listening notes

A portrait of August Jaeger, whose encouragement helped Elgar through years of discouragement. Listen for the quiet retreats between its broad orchestral swells. Select a note to hear its moment.
