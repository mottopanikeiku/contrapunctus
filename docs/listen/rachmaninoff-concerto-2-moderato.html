Chords before the tune

The concerto opens with repeated piano chords before its first extended melody. Listen for the growing weight of these widely spaced attacks and the low note beneath them, rather than expecting a tune immediately.

How we know

Placed from the score’s proportions; timing approximate. The solo chordal introduction is in the score. Relative level rises steadily across roughly 0:00–0:20, before a strong measured change around 0:24.

Around the music
After the First Symphony

The failure of Rachmaninoff’s First Symphony in 1897 damaged his confidence as a composer. The Second Concerto followed a period of difficulty in completing new work, not an uninterrupted sequence of public successes.

Listening notes

The piano begins alone with tolling chords, then gives the first melody to the orchestra. Listen for the changing division of labour: the soloist supplies accompaniment, sings, and turns the same material into a march. Select a note to hear its moment.
