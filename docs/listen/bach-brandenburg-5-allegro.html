Brandenburg Concerto No. 5, BWV 1050I. Allegro

1721, dedication · D major · Flute, solo violin, obbligato harpsichord, strings and continuo

A recurring orchestral frame

The opening establishes the D major ritornello: ensemble music that returns between solo episodes. Keep its coordinated rhythm in mind as the solo writing becomes more independent.

How we know

General description of this stretch. The concerto’s score establishes the ritornello design; this note describes the opening passage, not a timed entrance.

Around the music
A month in confinement

Bach accepted the Köthen court’s offer in 1717 before obtaining permission to leave Weimar. His employer had him confined for almost a month. Only after his dismissal could he take up the new post with Prince Leopold.

Listening notes

Bach gives flute, violin and harpsichord solo roles, then leaves the keyboard alone for an extended written-out cadenza. How does the balance change when one player supplies melody, bass and harmony? Select a note to hear its moment.
