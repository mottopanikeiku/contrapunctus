Remember the points of rest

The gavotte sets out two repeated strains before the six doubles, or variations. Remember where each phrase comes to rest as well as the tune itself: those resting places help connect the later, faster-moving writing to its model.

How we know

General description of this stretch. Nicolas Martello’s score discussion identifies the repeated two-part theme and the six variations. This cue describes that design rather than assigning individual cadences to seconds: https://www.edmu.fr/p/rameau.html

Around the music
Before the Paris operas

Rameau worked as an organist in several French cities, including Clermont, before settling in Paris in the early 1720s. His career as a church musician preceded his success at the Paris Opéra.

Listening notes

A gavotte becomes six variations, each retaining its harmonic route while changing the keyboard writing. Marcelle Meyer plays this harpsichord work on piano: follow the points of rest through the increasingly busy surface. Select a note to hear its moment.
