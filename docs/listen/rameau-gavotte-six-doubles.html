The dance before the variations

The gavotte lays out the melody and harmonic route that all six doubles will retain. Its two strains repeat: the first is eight bars, the second sixteen. Remember the cadence points as well as the tune; later, rapid notes will fill the spaces between them.

How we know

Around the music
Before the Paris operas

Rameau’s father was an organist in Dijon. Jean-Philippe also earned his living at church keyboards, including Clermont-Ferrand’s cathedral, before settling in Paris in the early 1720s. His first harpsichord book had appeared in 1706.

Listening notes

A gavotte becomes six variations, each preserving its harmonic route while changing what the hands must do. Marcelle Meyer plays the harpsichord score on piano, making the difference between held notes and rapid attacks especially clear. Select a note to hear its moment.
